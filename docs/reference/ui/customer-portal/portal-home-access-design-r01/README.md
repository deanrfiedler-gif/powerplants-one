# Portal home and access design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-eight images retain the Portal home and access design (CP-01) that Dean accepted on 23 October 2026. `/portal` is not built; the design defines the build. They cover:
- sign-in with the D-022 placeholder, first visit after an invitation, recovery and recovery email sent;
- Home for a site coordinator with one site, a CP1-only Home, the Help menu, Choose a site for a person with two sites, and Home after switching;
- Account and access, and access changed during the session;
- the staff side: the People record entry point and the Propose portal access dialog, an accepted amendment drawn on the CP-01 page only;
- tablet 1024, phone 390 and 320, three desktop states, seven phone states and the keyboard focus and field states board.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures D1–D19 are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/portal-home-access-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Image 27 is a frame holding copies of the seven phone state frames side by side.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, organisations, sites, requests, reports, visits and grants are synthetic fixtures and grant no access. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

The capture copies are duplicates of the accepted frames with two changes:
- The prototype reactions on 32 layers were removed, and the Captures page keeps its 113 flow starting points.
- Each of the 482 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` at its original size.

Three scans were run on the copies:
- After the swap, a scan found no Font Awesome instance and no visible vector outside a placeholder or the logo.
- A scan of 1,283 visible texts found none whose stored colour differed from its bound variable.
- No copy carries a prototype link.

The captures were exported with the Figma connector at scale 1 and downloaded directly into this folder.
