# Quotations and account design r01 — retained captures

These thirty-six images retain the Quotations and account design (CP-06) that Dean accepted on 30 October 2026. `/portal/commercial` is not built; the design defines the build. They cover:
- the commercial home;
- issued quotations: list, exact revision, reply and replaced;
- issued variations: list, view with reply, question and reply sent;
- orders and deliveries: in transit, delivered with proof, and report a problem;
- account, invoice query and access not granted;
- tablet 1024, phone 390 and 320, and eight phone states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures D1–D16 are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/quotations-account-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, organisations, sites, quotations, variations, dispatches, invoices and grants are synthetic fixtures and grant no access. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
