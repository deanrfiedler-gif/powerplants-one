# Support requests design r01 — retained captures

These thirty-five images retain the Support requests design (CP-02) that Dean accepted on 30 October 2026. `/portal/support` is not built; the design defines the build. They cover:
- the Support list, New request in three steps and a review, Received, the new request just received, and uncertain receipt;
- request detail: Waiting for you, a customer reply, and the later-stage Resolved and Visit booked views;
- the staff side: the public reply on a copy of the SV-02 record, an accepted amendment drawn on the CP-02 page only;
- tablet 1024, phone 390 and 320, two desktop states and nine phone states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures D1–D16 are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/support-requests-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 34 and 35 are frames holding copies of the phone state frames side by side.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, organisations, sites, equipment, requests, reports and visits are synthetic fixtures and grant no access. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

- After the swap, a scan found no Font Awesome instance and no visible vector outside a placeholder or a logo.
