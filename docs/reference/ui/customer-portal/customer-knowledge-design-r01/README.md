# Customer knowledge design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-nine images retain the Customer knowledge and guided support design (CP-05) that Dean accepted on 30 October 2026. `/portal/knowledge` is not built; the design defines the build. They cover:
- Help home, search results, the full article, and a superseded article opened from an old link;
- guided help questions, the guided result that asks for a technician, and CP-02 step 2 prefilled from guided help, drawn as a copy;
- tablet 1024 and phone 390 and 320;
- four desktop states and two phone states.

The decisions, conformance declaration, departures D1–D14 and a story difference from DK-05 are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/customer-knowledge-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Question 2 at 320 is not drawn.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, equipment, articles, guided paths and requests are synthetic fixtures. Steps, values and sources are placeholders, not validated procedures, and step images are placeholders. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
