# Deals design r02 — retained captures

These 28 images retain the Deals register design that Dean accepted on 24 October 2026, "Deals · CR-04", for the route `/sales/opportunities`. They cover:
- Board, List, Forecast and Archive at desktop 1440;
- the snapshot, filters, a two-row selection with its small panel, column settings, Add deal, Record Won, Won recorded with the handover prompt, Record Lost and a refused stage move;
- Pipeline settings, which stays a labelled proposal that is not in the app (capture 14);
- Board and List at tablet 1024;
- the list at phone 390 and 320 and the 320 Search and filter screen;
- nine states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/deals-figma-design.md#acceptance--24-october-2026). The 13–14 October r37 proposal images are the r01 evidence in [docs/testing/evidence/deals-figma](../../../../testing/evidence/deals-figma/README.md); they stay unchanged and predate R11. Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. The state frames are 1440 × 600 crops of the desktop page.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The deals, organisations, people, quotation and purchase-order references are synthetic fixtures; they are not adopted operating rules and grant no authority. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

The capture copies are duplicates of the accepted frames with two changes:
- Every prototype reaction was removed.
- Each of the 6,133 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components and the hidden ones, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` at its original size. A first pass made 351 hidden icons visible; it was discarded and redone, keeping each icon's visibility.

Two checks were run on the copies:
- A layer-by-layer comparison of each copy with its source frame found no difference in visibility, size or text outside the swapped icons.
- A scan found no Font Awesome instance and no prototype link. The only visible vectors outside a placeholder or the logo are the stage-header chevron edges (`Stage edge / outgoing point` and `Stage edge / incoming cut-in`), which are drawn shapes, not icons.

The captures were exported with the Figma connector at scale 1 and downloaded directly into this folder. Each file's PNG signature, dimensions and byte count were checked against its frame and the connector's report, and the SHA-256 values above were computed twice.
