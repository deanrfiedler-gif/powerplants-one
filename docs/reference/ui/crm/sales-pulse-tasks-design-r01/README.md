# Sales Pulse and Tasks design r01 — retained captures

These thirty images retain the Sales Pulse and Tasks design (`route:/sales/pulse`, `route:/sales/tasks`) that Dean accepted on 1 November 2026. They cover:
- Pulse, its activity window, Record outcome and its results, Plan activity, Plan next, the summary jump, outside access and a deals-only person, on desktop at 1440;
- the Tasks list, overdue filter, Reschedule and its failures, Set a date, Owner menu, Filters panel, Owner: Everyone, and Next page, on desktop at 1440;
- Pulse on tablet at 1024;
- Pulse, Tasks, the activity screen, sheets and states on phones at 390 and 320;
- the desktop states, and the row, control and keyboard states sheet.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/sales-pulse-tasks-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 23–29 are frames holding copies of several frames, 40 px apart on a grey background. Image 1 carries the purple annotation tags (D5–D11): review marks, not part of the page.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The customers, people, deals, calls and dates are synthetic. Images 21 and 29 (S8) illustrate a heavy week, not the story.

The copies were made on a temporary Figma page, which was deleted after export, so none stays in the file.
