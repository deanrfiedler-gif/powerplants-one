# Sales Pulse and Tasks design r02 — retained captures

These fifteen images retain the frames changed by the Q12 amendment, which Dean accepted on 1 November 2026: a value and expected close line on every Deal attention row. They keep their [r01](../sales-pulse-tasks-design-r01/README.md) numbers. Images not listed here are unchanged, so r01 remains their reference.

The decisions are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/sales-pulse-tasks-figma-design.md), section 9. Retain these bytes unchanged.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 23–29 are frames holding copies of several frames, 40 px apart on a grey background. Image 1 carries the purple annotation tags: review marks, not part of the page. Font Awesome icons are replaced by a plain placeholder square, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires. The customers, people, deals, calls and dates are synthetic. Image 29 (S8) illustrates a heavy week, not the story.
