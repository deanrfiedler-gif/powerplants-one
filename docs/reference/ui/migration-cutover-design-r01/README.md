# Migration and cutover design r01 — retained captures

These twenty images retain the Migration, coexistence and cutover design (AD-06) that Dean accepted on 1 November 2026. `/admin/migration` is not built, and the design defines the build. They cover:
- the migration overview;
- a full rehearsal run, reconciliation detail and the exceptions worklist;
- the coexistence plan, labelled "Proposed plan: not a decision";
- cutover readiness and a completed two-person sign-off;
- tablet 1024, phone 390 and 320, and eight states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures D1–D10 are in [the design decision](../../../decisions/migration-cutover-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, organisations, deals, snapshots, hashes, dates and references are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application. They authorise no migration, integration or cutover, and claim no production readiness or business acceptance.
