# Finance exceptions design r01 — retained captures

These twenty images retain the Finance exceptions design (FN-01 and FN-02, traced to FIN-03 and FIN-06) that Dean accepted on 1 November 2026. The page is not built and has no route. They cover:
- the worklist, by-owner view, difference record through to resolved, Needs Finance review and unknown outcome at desktop 1440;
- the worklist at tablet 1024;
- the worklist summary and exception record at phone 390 and 320;
- seven states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and D1–D10 are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/finance-exceptions-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, customers, documents, amounts and rules are synthetic fixtures. No figure is a posted amount, a credit decision or an operating rule.
