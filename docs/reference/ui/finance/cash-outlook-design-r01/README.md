# Cash outlook design r01 — retained captures

These thirty-five images retain the Cash outlook design (FN-05) that Dean accepted on 31 October 2026. `/finance/outlook` is not built; the design defines the build. They cover:
- the outlook overview, all expected lines, by project, week detail, assumptions and gaps, and waiting for me;
- scenario comparison, project outlook, source reads and actuals from MYOB;
- the side forms New scenario, Change an assumption and Confirm an expected date;
- tablet 1024, phone 390 and 320, and thirteen states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures D1–D15 are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/cash-outlook-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, organisations, projects, invoices, orders, receipts and scenarios are synthetic fixtures, and the date, staleness and GST rules are proposed, not verified. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
