# Knowledge design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-one images retain the Knowledge search and article detail design (`scope:DK-04`) that Dean accepted on 30 October 2026. They cover search with a five-value context, results with three separate statuses, an article at an exact revision, a superseded article and a suspected fix at desktop 1440 and tablet 1024; search, article and saved offline copies at phone 390 and 320; seven states; and the navigation placement.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/knowledge-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder. The earlier [HTML r01](../PPO-Knowledge-Search-and-Article-Detail-r01.html) and [report](../PPO-Knowledge-Search-and-Article-Detail-Report-r01.md) stay as earlier direction.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own.

The articles, steps, safety notes, sources, people, units and references are synthetic fixtures. They are not technical guidance or validated procedures, are not adopted operating rules and grant no authority. Each article image carries a purple design annotation saying so.
