# Estimate-to-actual review design r01 — retained captures

These 22 images retain the Estimate-to-actual review design (`/estimating/outcomes`, ES-09) that Dean accepted on 30 October 2026, at desktop 1440, tablet 1024 and phone 390 and 320. They cover the outcome register, the full outcome record, the comparison basis declaration, the line drill-down, the possible-duplicate block, review and sign-off, the ES-10 handover, the phone summary and approve screen, and nine states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/estimate-actual-review-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 02, 10 and 11 are full length; the other desktop frames are the 1440 × 900 viewport.

Every Font Awesome icon (640 in all) was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The people, organisations, estimates, quotations, amounts and dates are synthetic fixtures. No financial definition is adopted: margin is withheld because D-017 is open, materiality is per review and unadopted, and variance reasons are proposed codes. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
