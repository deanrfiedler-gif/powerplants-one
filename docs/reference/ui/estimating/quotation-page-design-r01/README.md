# Quotation page design r01 — retained captures

These 29 images retain the design Dean accepted on 30 October 2026: the customer quotation page at an indicative `/quote/[token]`, outside the application shell, and the staff side of Customer views. They cover the page at desktop 1440 and phone 390 and 320, its link states, the Send quotation composer on the release page's Distribution step, the Withdraw link confirmation, the Deal record and My Work amendments, and nine state cards.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/quotation-page-customer-views-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 11 and 09 are the same notice by design. The two Quotation detail frames copied unchanged onto the page are not recaptured here; they are retained in [Quotation detail design r01](../quotation-detail-design-r01/README.md).

Every Font Awesome icon (334 in all) was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The people, addresses (`*.example`), organisations, quotations, values and dates are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application; every send outcome and view shown is synthetic.
