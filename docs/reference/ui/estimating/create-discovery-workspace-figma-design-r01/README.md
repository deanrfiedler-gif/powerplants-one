# Create discovery workspace design r01 — retained captures

These eleven images retain the Create discovery workspace design (`route:/estimating/discovery/new`, scope ES-02) that Dean accepted on 18 October 2026. They cover choosing the opportunity, the first discovery from an accepted Sales brief, the review-and-create dialog, two desktop states, the tablet, phone 390 and 320, the four dialog outcomes and the phone loading state.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/create-discovery-workspace-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 02, 04 and 08 are full length. Image 10 is a frame holding copies of the four dialog state frames side by side.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The opportunities, customers, sites, Sales briefs and people are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

The capture copies are duplicates of the accepted frames with two changes:
- Interactions were removed from 66 layers (48 hover swaps, 12 variable sets, 6 conditionals and 2 links), and no flow starting point was left on a copy.
- Each of the 296 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` (`67:77`) at its original size. The copies hold 302 placeholders.

Three scans were run on the copies:
- After the swap, a scan found no Font Awesome instance. The radio rings and dots in the opportunity rows are plain ellipses, not icons.
- A scan of 937 visible texts found none whose stored colour differed from its bound variable.
- No copy carries a prototype link or a flow starting point.

The captures were exported with the Figma connector at scale 1 and downloaded directly into this folder.
