# Quotation detail design r01 — retained captures

These 22 images retain the Quotation detail design (`/estimating/quotes/[id]`, ES-05 · ES-06) that Dean accepted on 23 October 2026: the hub of one quotation across its life, at desktop 1440, tablet 1024 and phone 390 and 320. They cover the issued document and its customer page preview, revisions and their comparison, the send outcome, customer views and the privacy gate, the response, the follow-up, the history, a draft revision opened from Estimating, and six states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/quotation-detail-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 01 and 02 are full length; the other desktop frames are the 1440 × 900 viewport.

Every Font Awesome icon (777 in all) was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The people, addresses (`*.example`), organisations, quotations, values, hashes and dates are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application; every send outcome and view shown is synthetic.
