# Sales brief page design r01 — retained captures

These 25 images retain the Sales brief page design (`/sales/handoffs/estimating/[id]`, CR-02) that Dean accepted on 30 October 2026: both sides of one brief at desktop 1440, tablet 1024 and phone 390 and 320. They cover the Sales draft and its readiness checks, the frozen submission, the clarify, resolve, return and accept decisions, Source changed, the successor, the LC-13 workspace link and its recovery, History and the states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/sales-brief-page-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 01, 08, 11, 12 and 14 are full length; the other desktop frames are the 1440 × 900 viewport.

Every Font Awesome icon (723 in all) was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The data is synthetic.
