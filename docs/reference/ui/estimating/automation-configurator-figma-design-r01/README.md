# Automation Configurator design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-one images retain the Automation Configurator workbench design (`scope:ES-02`, `route:/estimating/discovery/[id]/nursery`) that Dean accepted on 22 October 2026. They cover the ten workbench sections at desktop 1440, four overlays, the entry from the Discovery workspace, the Overview at tablet 1024, seven phone frames at 390 and 320, and twelve states. The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/automation-configurator-figma-design.md). These images were re-exported on 24 October 2026, before merge, after Dean's post-acceptance audit corrected the findings counts, the Interfaces & site badge and the Overview frame height. Retain these bytes unchanged once merged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 19 and 20 are frames holding copies of the phone frames side by side.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The customer, site, scenarios, rates, limits and references are synthetic fixtures or unreviewed public observations and approve nothing. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
