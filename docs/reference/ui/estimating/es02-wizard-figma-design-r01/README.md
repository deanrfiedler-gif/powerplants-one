# ES-02 Estimation Wizard Figma design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-two images retain the Figma design of the Estimation Wizard discovery workspace (`/estimating/discovery/[id]`, ES-02) that Dean accepted on 12 October 2026. The design moves the page built to design board r01 onto the accepted Figma conventions: the labelled Shell v2 rail, the shared record header and view tabs, and the shared register grid. The decisions, the conformance declaration, the display rules and the differences the application should adopt are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/es02-wizard-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged. A revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame or board exported at scale 1:
- Images 01 and 07–18 are 1440 × 1000 frames, except image 07 at 1920 × 1200 and images 11–12, which are drawn at full length.
- Images 02–06 are the five steps at 1440 px wide, drawn at full length. In the design each step is a 1440 × 1000 frame whose content scrolls between the fixed header and the working-copy bar; image 01 is the 1000 px viewport of image 02.
- Image 19 is a board of the tablet frame at 1024 × 768 and five phone frames (four at 390 × 844, one at 320 × 700), with a label above each.
- Images 20 and 21 are boards of the seven full-size states and the catalogue of the other fourteen states.
- Image 22 is the board of the two family-colour options; option B was chosen.

Before export, every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The family icons, the rail icons, the check and the chevrons therefore appear as squares; the words beside them carry the meaning. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. Every figure is from the synthetic Northbank fixture of [build plan r04](../PPO-ES-02-Estimation-Wizard-Discovery-Alternatives-and-Revisions-Refinement-Build-Plan-r04.md) §4.9 and the design board's synthetic values. Alternative B's content in image 12 and alternatives D–J in image 21 are synthetic state data beyond that fixture. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
