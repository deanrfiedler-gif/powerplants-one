# Discovery costing design r01 — retained captures

These ten images retain the Discovery costing design (ES-02, `/estimating/discovery/[id]/costing`) that Dean accepted on 18 October 2026:
- the wizard's Pricing step for discovery r02 of Shade screen replacement, Block B, with cost lines for estimate v3, at desktop full length, tablet 1024 and phone 390 (also at full length) and 320;
- the review-and-adopt dialog;
- two page states: discovery changed while costing, and not ready to cost (Northbank r03 Incomplete);
- four dialog states and the phone loading state.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/discovery-costing-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Image 09 is a frame holding copies of the four dialog state frames side by side.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The workspaces, opportunities, customers, estimates, suppliers and people are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

The capture copies are duplicates of the accepted frames with two changes:
- Prototype links were removed.
- Each of the 273 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` at its original size. With the placeholders already in the frames, the copies hold 278 placeholders and five logos.

Three scans were run on the copies:
- After the swap, a scan found no Font Awesome instance and no visible vector outside a placeholder or the logo.
- A scan of 1,110 visible texts found none whose stored colour differed from its bound variable.
- No copy carries a prototype link or a flow starting point.

The captures were exported with the Figma connector at scale 1 and downloaded directly into this folder.
