# Site surveys design r01 — retained captures

These fourteen images retain the Site surveys design (CS-08) that Dean accepted on 19 October 2026. They cover two routes:
- `/surveys`: the register at desktop 1440 × 900, tablet 1024 and phone 390, and New survey on desktop;
- `/surveys/[id]`: the survey "Bay 3–4 irrigation as-found" with its Survey scope, Observations, Photos, Follow-up and Review & handover tabs on desktop, the handover once reviewed, Survey scope on phone at 390 and 320, and recording an observation on phone in a successor draft.

Four phone states are included. The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/site-surveys-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Image 14 is a frame holding copies of the four phone state frames side by side.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The surveys, sites, people, readings, file names and hashes are synthetic fixtures; the photo tiles are neutral placeholders, not photographs. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

The capture copies are duplicates of the accepted frames with two changes:
- Prototype links were removed, and no flow starting point was added to the Captures page.
- Each of the 505 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` at its original size. With the placeholders already in the frames, the copies hold 509 visible placeholders and nine logos.

Three scans were run on the copies:
- After the swap, a scan found no Font Awesome instance and no visible vector outside a placeholder or the logo.
- A scan of 1,311 visible texts found none whose stored colour differed from its bound variable.
- No copy carries a prototype link.

The captures were exported with the Figma connector at scale 1 and downloaded directly into this folder.
