# Facilities and growing areas design r01 — retained captures

These seventeen images retain the Facilities and growing areas design (CS-05) that Dean accepted on 18 October 2026. They cover four routes:
- `/facilities`: the register at desktop 1440 × 900 (list, the Bay 4 snapshot and the Hierarchy view), tablet 1024 and phone 390;
- `/facilities/[id]`: the record for Bay 4, with the Overview and Equipment tabs on desktop, and the Overview on phone at 390 and 320;
- `/facilities/[id]/edit`: Correct details and the review of saved and proposed values on desktop, and the review on phone;
- `/facilities/new`: Add facility or area on desktop.

Three phone states and three desktop states are included. The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/facilities-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Image 14 is a frame holding copies of the three phone state frames side by side.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The sites, facilities, equipment, people and records are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

The capture copies are duplicates of the accepted frames with two changes:
- Prototype links were removed.
- Each of the 794 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` at its original size. With the placeholders already in the frames, the copies hold 810 placeholders and twelve logos.

Three scans were run on the copies:
- After the swap, a scan found no Font Awesome instance and no visible vector outside a placeholder or the logo.
- A scan of 2,299 visible texts found none whose stored colour differed from its bound variable.
- No copy carries a prototype link or a flow starting point.

The captures were exported with the Figma connector at scale 1 and downloaded directly into this folder.
