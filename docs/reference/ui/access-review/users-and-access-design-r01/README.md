# Users and access design r01 — retained captures

These thirteen images retain the Users and access design (AD-01) that Dean accepted on 19 October 2026. They cover six routes:
- `/admin/access`: the identity register at desktop 1440 × 900, tablet 1024 and phone 390;
- `/admin/access/[id]`: Sasha Cole's person access with the Grants and Check eligibility tabs on desktop, and grants by scope on phone at 390 and 320;
- `/admin/access/[id]/roles`: Compare bundle, Service coordinator r02 at SYN-A;
- `/admin/access/capabilities`: the capability catalogue;
- `/admin/access/requests`: the requests register and the Propose one additional capability dialog;
- `/admin/access/requests/[id]`: request SYN-PPO-ACR-000004 with the independent decision.

Five phone states are included. The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/users-and-access-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Image 13 is a frame holding copies of the five phone state frames side by side.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The identities, grants, requests, companies and sites are synthetic fixtures and grant no access. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

- The 226 prototype reactions were removed, and the Captures page keeps its 103 flow starting points.
- Each of the 588 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` at its original size.

Three scans were run on the copies:
- After the swap, a scan found no Font Awesome instance and no visible vector outside a placeholder or the logo.
- A scan of 1,553 visible texts found none whose stored colour differed from its bound variable.
- No copy carries a prototype link.

The captures were exported with the Figma connector at scale 1 and downloaded directly into this folder.
