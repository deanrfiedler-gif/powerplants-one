Sites, structures and growing areas

Organisation → addressed sites → exact facilities and growing areas, with separate equipment service relationships.

Physical location

An organisation can have multiple sites. Each site carries its own address, time zone and access/delivery context. Facilities and growing areas carry their local identity and position on that site.

May be nestedGreenhouse → compartments
May belong directly to siteOutdoor Field A
Structure and use are separatePropagation ≠ construction type
Non-growing facilities are validPack Room · Irrigation Shed

Reuse canonical Facility/Asset identities where appropriate. Names may repeat at different sites; parent changes retain history. Area totals must not double-count a parent footprint and its contained areas.

What the equipment serves

A pump physically installed in Irrigation Shed 01 may supply Block A and Block B. Record one pump, its installation location and separate served-area links.

An irrigation block can be a physical outdoor area or an irrigation/control grouping. A service grouping does not replace the physical hierarchy.

Controller zones, climate compartments, sensors and programme references need explicit relationships. Any new join, field or permission requires a defined contract; this register does not assert those features already work.

The six required facility examples

Greenhouse

Structure on the site; optional compartments/growing areas. Capture actual type, cladding, dimensions and units where known; keep use and crop separate.

Tunnel

Named tunnel or tunnel block. Use the approved Polytunnel value where it matches the structure; show a recognisable Tunnel label and relevant cover/count fields.

Propagation House

Propagation function with its actual structure recorded separately: greenhouse, tunnel, shade structure or room. Seasonal crop context may be dated without defining the structure.

Pack Room

Non-growing packing/receiving facility, standalone or inside a structure. Record local position and access without requiring crop fields.

Irrigation Block / Field

Named outdoor field/area directly under the site, or a defined irrigation grouping linked to served areas. Record area and units where known; distinguish physical boundaries from control groupings.

Irrigation Shed

Non-growing equipment structure/room. Link installed pumps, controllers and fertigation equipment as Assets, keeping physical position separate from served growing areas.

Keep scope proportionate. PPO’s core brief supports the horticulture supplier, engineering, project and service operation. Grower crop-production/traceability and water/energy analysis are visible conditional expansions (CX-05/CX-06), requiring a confirmed business case and source.
