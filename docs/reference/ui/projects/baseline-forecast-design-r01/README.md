# Baseline, forecast and dependency review design r01 — retained captures

These sixteen images retain the Baseline, forecast and dependency review design (PJ-03) that Dean accepted on 19 October 2026. They extend the Schedule and Baselines views of `/projects/[id]` and the Programme at `/projects/programme`.

- **Desktop 1440:**
  - the Schedule with baseline r1 shown, and the Display menu;
  - the task panel, for a forecast change and for recording actual dates;
  - Baselines in working days, in the viewport and full length;
  - the Working calendar drawer, in view and change modes;
  - the Programme.
- **Tablet 1024:** the Schedule.
- **Phone:** the Schedule list at 390 and 320, the Actual dates sheet, Baselines and Working calendar.
- **States:** thirteen cards.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/baseline-forecast-figma-design.md). The phone frames use the compact phone header of the Phone header amendment. Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Capture 06 is full length.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The people, organisations, projects, sites, calendar exceptions and dates are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
