# Contract obligations, notices and variations design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-one images retain the Contract obligations, notices and variations design (PJ-05) that Dean accepted on 16 October 2026. The design covers `/projects/commercial` (menu label "Variations & obligations"), the variation, obligation and basis records, and a Commercial view on the project record, at desktop 1440, tablet 1024 and phone 390 and 320. The decisions, conformance declaration, display rules and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/project-commercial-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Image 21 is a frame holding copies of the fourteen state cards at their positions in the States section. Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The project, quotation, contract, people, dates and amounts are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application; no PJ-05 page is built.

The capture copies are duplicates of the accepted frames with two changes:
- Prototype links were removed.
- Each of the 564 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` at its original size.

Two scans were run on the copies:
- After the swap, a scan found no Font Awesome instance left in any copy.
- A scan of 2,175 visible texts found none whose stored colour differed from its bound variable.

Each copy was exported with the Figma connector and downloaded directly to this folder. Image 19 was exported again after its two sheet buttons were centred in both the accepted frame and the copy; the hash above is the final export.
