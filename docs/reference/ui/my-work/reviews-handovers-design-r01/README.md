# Reviews & handovers design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-four images retain the Reviews & handovers design amendment (SH-06) that Dean accepted on 1 November 2026. The route exists in an earlier layout; this design is not built. They cover:
- the six views and filters at desktop 1440;
- the queue and the detail overlay at tablet 1024;
- list, detail and Back to list at phone 390 and 320, with the view chooser and filters at 390;
- six states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and D1–D14 are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/reviews-handovers-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. The node is the accepted frame on page `2998:12031`.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, records, ages and due dates are synthetic fixtures. No value is a deadline, an authority or an operating rule.
