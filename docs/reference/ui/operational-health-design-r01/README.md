# Operational health design r01 — retained captures

These thirty-three images retain the Operational health design (AD-09) that Dean accepted on 31 October 2026. `/foundation` and `/admin` are built; the Overview, Incidents and Release readiness views are not, and the design defines the build. They cover:
- the health overview;
- the incidents register, an incident record and Close with reason;
- the Exceptions & recovery register and a recovery item with a safe retry;
- Recovery readiness: backup and restore evidence, procedures and known limitations;
- the release readiness review: Not ready, Sign off, Ready for demo upgrade and After the upgrade;
- Foundation checks;
- tablet 1024, phone 390 and 320, and twelve states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures D1–D16 are in [the design decision](../../../decisions/operational-health-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, incidents, reports, releases, run results and migration numbers are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application, and they claim no production readiness, real uptime or business acceptance.
