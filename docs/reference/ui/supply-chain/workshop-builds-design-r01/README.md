# Workshop builds design r01 — retained captures

These sixteen images retain the Workshop builds design (CX-02, the first of the three workshop pages) that Dean accepted on 19 October 2026. They cover the proposed `/workshop` register and `/workshop/[id]` build record under the Supply chain rail's proposed "Workshop" item: six desktop 1440 frames (four at full length), the tablet 1024 register, four phone frames at 390 and 320, and five phone states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/workshop-builds-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The people, projects, builds, serial numbers, measurements and dates are synthetic fixtures; no MYOB figure is shown. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
