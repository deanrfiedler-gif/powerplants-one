# Supply and Finance trust design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-one images of nineteen frames retain the Supply and Finance trust and exceptions design (`scope:RP-05`) that Dean accepted on 1 November 2026. They cover:
- the report at the planned route `/reports/reconciliation` on desktop at 1440 and tablet at 1024;
- REP-09 drilled to the blocked cable kit, REP-10 three-way ageing, the REP-11 trust panel with exceptions, KPI-13 withheld, and the export side form;
- summaries and one drill list on phones at 390 and 320;
- eight states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/supply-finance-trust-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size, except the two full-length reports. The export is capped at 4,096 px, so each report is split into two images at section boundaries (01a and 01b, 07a and 07b).

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The materials, returns, claims, credits, measures, operations, people, dates and figures are synthetic fixtures; they are not adopted operating rules and grant no authority. The images show REP-09 to REP-11 as published v1 definitions, which exist in the synthetic story only; KPI-13 and KPI-14 are shown without a published definition. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
