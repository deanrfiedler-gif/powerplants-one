# Service performance design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-two images of eighteen frames retain the Service, maintenance and resource performance design (`scope:RP-04`) that Dean accepted on 1 November 2026. They cover:
- the report at the planned route `/reports/service` on desktop at 1440 and tablet at 1024;
- the KPI-01 drill, KPI-04 median and tail, the REP-12 maintenance list, KPI-06 with Definition pending, and the export side form;
- the field readiness summary and one drill list on phones at 390 and 320;
- seven states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/service-performance-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size, except the two full-length reports. The export is capped at 4,096 px, so each report is split into three images at section boundaries (01a–01c and 07a–07c).

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The visits, packs, people, dates and figures are synthetic fixtures; they are not adopted operating rules and grant no authority. Unless an image says Definition pending, it shows definitions as adopted v1, and none is published yet. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
