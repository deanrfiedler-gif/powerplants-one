# Sales performance design r01 — retained captures

These twenty-one images of seventeen frames retain the Sales and quotation performance design (`scope:RP-02`) that Dean accepted on 30 October 2026. They cover:
- the report at the planned route `/reports/sales` on desktop at 1440 and tablet at 1024;
- the drill-down from REP-04 to its quotations, REP-02 with Definition pending, filters open and the export side form;
- section summaries and one drill-down list on phones at 390 and 320;
- seven states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/sales-performance-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size, except the two full-length reports. The export is capped at 4,096 px, so each report is split into three images at section boundaries (01a–01c and 06a–06c).

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The deals, leads, quotations, accounts, approvals, people, dates and figures are synthetic fixtures; they are not adopted operating rules and grant no authority. Unless an image says Definition pending, it shows definitions as published v1 and a synthetic test pricing policy, and neither exists yet (decision D15). The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.
