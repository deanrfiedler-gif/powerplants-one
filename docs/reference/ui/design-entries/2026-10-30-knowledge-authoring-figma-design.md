**Knowledge authoring design, 30 October 2026:** Dean accepted the Figma design for DK-05 (`/knowledge/review`). Each hand-in is a learning item with its source, never an article. Review is independent of the author, a hypothesis becomes a procedure only through validation with evidence, publishing creates an exact revision, an edit after publication creates a successor, and withdrawal keeps history. The [decision](../../../decisions/knowledge-authoring-figma-design.md) records it, and the [retained captures](../knowledge-authoring/knowledge-authoring-design-r01/README.md) hold the evidence. Nothing is built.
