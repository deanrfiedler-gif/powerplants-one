**Project financial performance design, 19 October 2026:** Dean accepted the Figma design for FN-03 (`/finance/projects`) at desktop, tablet and phone, with seven states, after a read-only look at MYOB project screens. MYOB stays the ledger: the page reads budget, actual and committed cost, invoices and receipts, and adds the monthly review, cost by category, over/under billing and owned differences. A figure with an incomplete or misaligned input shows why instead of a number. The [decision](../../../decisions/project-financial-performance-figma-design.md) records it, and the [retained captures](../finance/project-performance-design-r01/README.md) are twenty-two icon-free PNGs with hashes.
