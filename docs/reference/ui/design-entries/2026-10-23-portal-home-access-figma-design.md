**Portal home and access design, 23 October 2026:** Dean accepted the Figma design for CP-01 (`/portal`) at desktop, tablet and phone, with seven phone states and three desktop states. The portal has its own shell, shows one organisation and site at a time and never mixes records across sites, and grants access only through a staff proposal that is independently reviewed and applied. Sign-in, the portal's name and retention stay open (D-022, D-024, D-012). The [decision](../../../decisions/portal-home-access-figma-design.md) records it, and the [retained captures](../customer-portal/portal-home-access-design-r01/README.md) are twenty-eight icon-free PNGs with hashes. Listing it here does not repeat the dated inventory audit.
