**Users and access design, 19 October 2026:** Dean accepted the Figma design for AD-01 (`/admin/access` and its person, roles, capabilities and requests routes) at desktop, tablet and phone, with five phone states. Grants are grouped by company, with the ERP key beside same-name companies, and by site; an eligibility check names the nearest current grant when it fails; approval of an access request changes no access. The [decision](../../../decisions/users-and-access-figma-design.md) records it, and the [retained captures](../access-review/users-and-access-design-r01/README.md) are thirteen icon-free PNGs with hashes. The app still shows the native AD-01 pages. Listing it here does not repeat the dated inventory audit.
