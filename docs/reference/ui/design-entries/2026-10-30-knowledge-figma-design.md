**Knowledge design, 30 October 2026:** Dean accepted the Figma design for DK-04 at desktop, tablet and phone, with seven states. Validation, review and applicability always show separately, in words. Suspected fixes stay hypotheses with no steps, superseded articles name their successor and withhold their steps, and reading, saving or feedback never validates anything. Authoring, review, publication and withdrawal stay with DK-05. The [decision](../../../decisions/knowledge-figma-design.md) records departures D1–D12 and the answers to Q1–Q6. The captures are design compositions, not application captures, and set no UI baseline.
