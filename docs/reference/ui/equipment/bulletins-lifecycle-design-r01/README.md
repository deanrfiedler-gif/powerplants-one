# Bulletins and lifecycle design r01 — retained captures

These fourteen images retain the Service bulletin applicability and Support lifecycle design (`scope:EQ-06`, `scope:EQ-07`) that Dean accepted on 31 October 2026. They cover:
- the Bulletins register, the bulletin record, the asset review form and a superseded bulletin, on desktop at 1440;
- the Lifecycle register, an asset's support evidence, the handover to Sales aftercare and its read-back, on desktop at 1440;
- the Bulletins register on tablet at 1024;
- the on-site check and review on phones at 390 and 320;
- eight states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/bulletins-lifecycle-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 10–14 are frames holding copies of several frames, 40 px apart on a grey background: side by side, except image 12, which is stacked.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The customers, people, manufacturers, bulletins, references and dates are synthetic fixtures; they are not adopted operating rules, make no claim about any real product and grant no authority. These are design compositions, not application captures.
