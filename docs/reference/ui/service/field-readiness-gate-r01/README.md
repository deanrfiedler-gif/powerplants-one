# Field readiness gate A1 r01 — retained captures

These twelve images retain the field readiness gate amendment that Dean accepted on 16 October 2026 ("I am happy to proceed with all six based on your recommendations", [PPO-UXB-DEC-01](../../../../decisions/ux-benchmark-decisions-2026-10-16.md) decision 4). Images 01 to 07 are section A1 on the accepted Job pack page and record amendment A06 in [the Job pack design decision](../../../../decisions/job-pack-figma-design.md) section 8. Images 08 to 10 are section A1 on the accepted My jobs page and record amendment A07 in [the My jobs design decision](../../../../decisions/my-jobs-figma-design.md). Images 11 and 12 are exemplar E7, the parts-readiness ladder of UXB-10, which the first build does not implement. The proposal, its rules and its departures A1-D1 to A1-D5 are in [the field readiness gate record](../../../../decisions/field-readiness-gate-proposal.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. Visits, work orders, packs, people, sites, parts and times are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

The capture copies are duplicates of the accepted frames with two changes:
- Prototype links were removed, and the flow starts the copies would have added to the Captures page were dropped.
- Each of the 626 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` at its original size.

A scan after the swap found no Font Awesome instance left in any copy, no prototype link, and no copy overlapping another node on the page. Each copy was exported with the Figma connector at scale 1, downloaded directly to this folder and inspected.

Images 04 and 07 show customer site readiness (P4) and image 04 shows SYN-PPO-P04 v2 with ten criteria. The first build implements P1 to P3 only: policy v2 carries nine criteria, and site readiness and the issue lead time follow in a later increment. The field readiness gate record, section 10, lists what is built and what is not.
