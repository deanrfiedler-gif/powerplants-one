# Time & labour review design r01 — retained captures

These thirty-four images retain the Time & labour review design (FI-01, coordinator side, traced to SVC-08) that Dean accepted on 31 October 2026. The page is not built and has no route. They cover:
- the register, visit record, query and reply, adjustment, billable marking, accept, send, history, allowance, planner hand-off and four exception views at desktop 1440;
- the visit record at tablet 1024;
- the technician's question, reply and read-only time at phone 390 and 320;
- ten states.

Options F1–F3 were drawn after the acceptance, await Dean's review and are not in this set.

The decisions, conformance declaration and D1–D19 are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/time-labour-review-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../../ppo-logo-pack/README.md). The people, sites, visits, hours, quoted labour, thresholds and handoffs are synthetic fixtures. No figure is a quote, a rate or an operating rule.
