# Customer response design r01 — retained captures

These fourteen images retain the Customer response design (FI-07) that Dean accepted on 22 October 2026. FI-07 has no route of its own: the images show the Customer response tab of the report record at `/service/reports/[id]` on desktop 1440 × 900 and tablet 1024, the focused presentation view, and the technician's phone view of the same report at 390 and 320.

Five phone states are included. The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/customer-response-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Image 14 is a frame holding copies of the five phone state frames side by side.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The reports, people, times, hashes and the signature mark are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

The capture copies are duplicates of the accepted frames with two changes:
- Prototype links were removed, and no flow starting point was added to the Captures page.
- Each of the 376 Font Awesome icon instances, including those nested in shared components, was swapped for `Icon/placeholder` at its original size. The copies hold 374 visible placeholders and eight logos.

Three scans were run on the copies:
- After the swap, a scan found no Font Awesome instance and no visible vector outside a placeholder, the logo or the drawn synthetic signature line.
- A scan of 1,031 visible texts found none whose stored colour differed from its bound variable.
- No copy carries a prototype link.

The captures were exported with the Figma connector at scale 1 and downloaded directly into this folder.
