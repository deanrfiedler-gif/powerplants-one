# Sales aftercare design r01 — retained captures

These eleven images retain the Sales aftercare and renewal worklist design (`scope:CR-05`) that Dean accepted on 30 October 2026. They cover:
- the worklist, the customer review with its outcome ledger, the Complete review dialog, the Service referral, the training record, the renewal handover and the history, on desktop at 1440;
- the worklist on tablet at 1024;
- the review record and the Record a statement form on phones at 390 and 320;
- eight states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/sales-aftercare-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Images 09–11 are frames holding copies of several frames side by side, 40 px apart on a grey background.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The customers, people, statements, references and dates are synthetic fixtures; they are not adopted operating rules and grant no authority. These are design compositions, not application captures.
