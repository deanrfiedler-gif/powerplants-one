# Inspections design r01 — retained captures

These twelve images retain the Inspections design (FI-03 and FI-04) that Dean accepted on 18 October 2026. They cover two routes:
- `/my-jobs/inspections` (FI-03): the technician's capture on desktop 1440 × 900 and phone 390 and 320, My inspections on a phone, the submit sheet and three phone states. Images 05, 07–10 and 12.
- `/service/inspections` (FI-04): the review register at desktop 1440 × 900 and tablet 1024, the attempt record awaiting review, returned and accepted, and the reviewer's Retests tab on a phone. Images 01–04, 06 and 11.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/inspections-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. Image 12 is a frame holding copies of the three state frames side by side on the canvas grey.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own. The people, sites, equipment, readings, instruments, defects and times are synthetic fixtures. The images are design compositions, not captures of the application.

Before the captures were committed, the writers of this record found two slips in the accepted frames. Claude corrected them in Figma on 18 October 2026, rebuilt the affected capture copies and exported images 05, 07 and 12 again; the table above gives the new sizes and hashes. Nothing else changed.
- The account button on the technician frames (`1339:317844`, `1339:317656`) read "SC", Sasha Cole, the reviewer. It now reads "RT", Riley Tran, the technician.
- The "can't record yet, not attending" state (`1344:4086`) said "nothing saved" but still listed two recorded checks with times and a typed torque value. It now reads "Checks · none recorded", each check reads "Not recorded", and the times and value are cleared.
- The "not connected" state (`1344:3746`) now shows Save draft and Submit dimmed, matching the incident report and the rule that a connection failure disables new commands.
