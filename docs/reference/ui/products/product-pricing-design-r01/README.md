# Product pricing design r01 — retained captures

These thirty-five images retain the Product pricing design (PD-03) that Dean accepted on 31 October 2026. `/products/pricing` is built, but has not moved to this design. They cover ten desktop frames, two frames of additions, tablet 1024, six phone frames at 390 and 320, and sixteen states.

The decisions, conformance declaration and departures D1–D15 are in [the design decision](../../../../decisions/product-pricing-figma-design.md). Retain these bytes unchanged; a revised design receives a new revision folder.

Each image is one Figma frame exported at scale 1 at its exact frame size. The story is synthetic. S6 and the USD row in S7 are illustrative.

Every Font Awesome icon was replaced by a plain placeholder square before export, as [ADR-0053](../../../../decisions/ADR-0053-figma-page-design.md) requires, so no Pro artwork is in this repository. The Powerplants logo is the company's own, from the [logo pack r01](../../ppo-logo-pack/README.md).
