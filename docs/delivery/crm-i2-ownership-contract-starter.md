## Included contract questions

Resolve these from actual accepted instructions/contracts where possible. Where a material business choice remains unmade, finish the concrete proposal and comparison before asking Dean to decide; do not invent managerial authority or request approval again for a decision already made.

1. **Who may initiate?** Distinguish the current owner, another scoped sales actor and any proposed manager. Existing create/edit/read capability alone is not evidence that a manager or transfer grant exists. Specify the smallest explicit proposed authority and scope, its rationale, and the decision still needed.
2. **Who may receive?** Define eligibility from actual current company/site/organisation/person, CRM and Activity contracts. Include inactive, Systems, unassigned, other-company, site-limited and partially revoked identities. An owner label must never supply a permission grant.
3. **What changes?** Only the accountable opportunity owner, aggregate version and append-only handover evidence would change in the proposed command. Preserve stage/outcome, fixed customer/site/contact context and original authorship. Treat the designated Activity's owner separately; do not silently transfer actions, estimates, quotations, service work or Finance records.
4. **What is retained?** Specify original/current owners, initiator, reason, server time, expected version, operation identity and receipt/hash/event evidence. Define authorised original-operation recovery after transfer, lost responses and grant revocation without leaking historical private content or replaying a new effect.
5. **What does the user see?** Propose a labelled canonical-detail form, current and proposed owner comparison, full next-action ownership consequences, validation and deliberate conflict/retry. Board/Grid remains a read presentation of the resulting server records. No drag/drop, bulk transfer, reassociation, extra stage or close/reopen is included.
6. **How is it proved?** Author a future DB/HTTP/browser/restart matrix for transfer competing with qualification/action planning/another transfer; former/new-owner commands; direct/list/search/selector/count/receipt scope; all-target Activity visibility; immutable history and original recovery; identity change; no duplicate allocation/effect. Identify fresh/upgrade/reseed/FK evidence a future additive migration would need while preserving all accepted suites.
