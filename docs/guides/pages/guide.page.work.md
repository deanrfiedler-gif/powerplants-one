Your activities, reviews and follow-ups from every department in one place, with what is late at the top.

## What you see {#overview}

My Work gathers the work you own or follow, so you can see what is late, what is due today and what is waiting on someone else. It shows only records your access covers.

### The summary strip

Counts what is overdue, due today, waiting on someone else, and deals with no next activity. Select a count to see those items.

### My activities

Lists your overdue and today's activities, most urgent first.

### Today's schedule

Shows your appointments for today. Times are in AEST (UTC+10).

### Waiting on others

Lists requests you are following up.

### Needs a next activity

Lists your open deals with nothing planned. A banner appears when activities still need a due date.

## How to use it {#how}

1. **Finish an activity**\
   Select the circle at the start of its row, then record the outcome. On a phone, open the activity and select Complete.
2. **Move it**\
   Open the row's actions menu and select Reschedule. On a phone, open the activity first. Choose a new date and time.
3. **Open it**\
   Select its title to see the contact details, the record it belongs to and its history.
4. **Add one**\
   Select Activity, or the + button on a phone.
5. **Set missing dates**\
   Select Set dates in the banner.

## Your journey {#journey}

Views and filters change what the overview shows; the side menu opens the other My Work pages.

1. **Choose whose work**\
   Choose My work or Everyone I can see, if your access allows it.
2. **Narrow the list**\
   Filters narrow by type and source.
3. **Save a view**\
   Save a combination as a view and pin it in the side menu.
4. **Choose the cards**\
   Open More actions, then Customise overview to choose which cards appear.
5. **Go further**\
   The side menu opens My actions (everything, not only today), Reviews & handovers, Blocked & waiting, Team queue, and Updates & preferences.

## On your phone {#mobile}

On a phone the same work appears as Quick actions, Needs attention, a weekly agenda and Waiting on others. The + button adds an activity, and the My Work menu opens the other pages.

## Keyboard shortcuts {#shortcuts}

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl K` | Search |
| `Esc` | Close a panel |

## If something is unavailable {#recovery}

If a count looks too low, the footer shows when the page was last updated: select Refresh, or reload the page. If something is missing, remember you see only records your access covers; if you work in one company, items from other companies are not shown, and you can change that in the account panel. If a source is unavailable, the page names it and keeps the rest. Nothing missing is counted as zero.

> [!NOTE]
> My Work lists and links your work. Each record's own page decides what you can change, and checks your access again when you save.
