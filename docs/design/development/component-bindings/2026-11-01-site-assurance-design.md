## Register table

Site assurance uses the grid register with two-line cells, because a check needs its reference, reader or limit under its name. Rows run about 60 px, status is always in words, and group bands span the Select and Settings columns. The overview's Next step and Owner columns scroll sideways at 1440 with the side menu open. The Figma parts are in the [accepted design](../../../decisions/site-assurance-figma-design.md), and nothing is built.
