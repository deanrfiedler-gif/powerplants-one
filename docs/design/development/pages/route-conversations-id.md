# Conversation thread — design reference

Stable entry: `route:/conversations/[id]`. Owner: Dean Fiedler. Status: **Draft for visual review**.
Scope: PPO-AI-THREADS-PROP, step T1, slices T1c-1 and T1c-3 ([T1c design](../../../decisions/conversations-t1c-design.md), section 11 for the context panel; [T1 scope](../../../decisions/conversations-t1-scope.md)). People-only; no Carl, AI, voice, branches, sharing, pins or exclusions.

## Purpose and task

Read and answer one conversation about a deal or project, and close the questions you were asked.

1. Read the messages and any ask card waiting on you.
2. Reply in the composer; a reply by the asked person closes their open asks in the thread.
3. Choose No answer needed, Resolve or Reopen as the conversation requires.
4. Check the context panel for the record, its documents, contacts and related records, and who owes the open questions.

## Desktop

The sidebar stays beside the thread with the open row marked current. Header: title, participant initials, state tag (Waiting on you, Open or Resolved), the record link, "Main thread · started by …, {time}", and Resolve or Reopen for the creator and participants; the "…" menu is hidden. Messages show author and time (no role line: the read carries none), `@` tags as names in link blue, and `#` links as record-link pills drawn from the reader's access; one the reader cannot open reads "A record you can't open" with no action. Ask cards sit under the message that made them. The composer has the `@` and `#` pickers, an Ask switch per tagged person (off by default) with a short question and an optional reply-by date, and Send (Ctrl+Enter). The breadcrumb reads "Conversations / {record number}". From 1280 px the context panel (T1c-3) is docked at 320 px to the right of the thread: "Context", "Rebuilt from the records each time it opens, from what you can open.", Close, then the collapsible groups Record, Files and documents, Contacts, Related records and This thread. Each item has a grey reason pill ("Linked on the deal", "Mentioned by Alex", "Asked by Sam"). Closing it puts a Context button beside Resolve and moves keyboard focus to it. The page owns and consumes that one-shot focus request; later button mounts on a viewport change do not steal focus. Repeated close/reopen cycles remain keyboard accessible. From 781 to 1279 px the Context button opens the same panel as a modal drawer at the right (320 px, full height, Escape and backdrop close, focus returns to the button). Verified at 1440 × 900 and 1024 × 768.

## Mobile

At 780 px and narrower the thread is full screen with "Back to conversations"; ask cards lose their indent and the title row wraps. The Context button opens the panel as a bottom sheet with a handle (up to 85 % of the height); at 360 px and narrower the button is icon-only and keeps the name "Context". Verified at 390 × 844 and 320 × 844.

## Shared components and states

`RecordLink` (with `Project` added), `RecordIdentity`, `Button`, `ErrorNotice`, `ProductIcon` (chevron, close), `useRecoverableCommand` (posting, resolve, reopen, No answer needed; one journalled operation per key), plain `api()` for mark-read. The context panel (`context-panel.tsx`) reads `GET /api/v1/conversations/[id]/context` and uses a native modal `<dialog>` for the drawer and sheet, following the WorklistPanel pattern (Escape, focus return); it is page-scoped, not a shared component. States: loading skeleton; a 403 or 404 read shows only "This conversation isn't available to you" and drops the thread, so a later re-read cannot draw it again; another read error keeps the last thread with "This conversation could not be refreshed. Showing what was last loaded." and Retry; a state command or a post refused because the thread changed (version conflict, resolved thread, closed ask) re-reads it, a state command with "This conversation changed while you were reading it. It has been refreshed."; a failed send keeps the text and tags with Retry; a picker that fails shows Retry and the message can still be sent; a resolved thread replaces the composer with "Reopen this conversation to reply"; a refused send (403) shows "You can read this conversation but not post in it." Context panel states: a loading skeleton; a read error with Retry; a failed re-read keeps the last panel with "The context could not be refreshed. Showing what was last loaded." and Retry; a 403 or 404 removes the docked panel and closes an open drawer or sheet; an empty group says so ("No related records you can open."). Drafts are held per thread above the thread's read, so they survive the 30-second re-read, a failed re-read and a resolve elsewhere. A Resolve, Reopen or No answer needed that was not confirmed shows "… has not been confirmed." with Retry and disables the other actions. Tags are atomic: an edit that touches a tag removes the whole tag, so a label never remains as plain text; a typed `@` or `#` opens the picker and stays if the picker is dismissed.

## Behaviour, handovers and verification

The record link opens `/crm/opportunities/{id}` or `/projects/{id}`. Reading marks the thread read for participants only. The context panel is a view rebuilt for the viewer on every read (S5) from existing reads only, and leaves out anything the viewer cannot open, title included. Its record link opens `/sales/opportunities/{id}` or `/projects/{id}`; a quotation opens `/estimating/quotes/{id}`; a library document opens its linked revision from the document library; a contact opens `/people/{id}` with phone and email only when the viewer may read the person.
