## Carried over from the adopted trial (unchanged)

- **C1** Desktop rail collapsed by default and 240 px expanded; the logo, the navigation glyphs and the footer arrows share one column. The trial fixed the collapsed width at 72 px and the column at x = 36; on 8 November the rail widened to 80 px and the column moved to x = 40 (D10).
- **C2** Navy rail (brand/navy) under a deep navy top bar (brand/navy-deep); no green fill behind navy text. Active workspace = slate tile (surface/rail-active) with a 3 px brand/green left bar; keyboard focus = a separate 2 px white outline. (The rail tone later moved to surface/rail, R105.)
- **C3** Settings and the 44 px expand/collapse action stay fixed below the scrolling workspace list; tooltips read Expand navigation / Collapse navigation; both widths preserve the page and its state.
- **C4** The workspace flyout opens by click; Pin docks it at 280 px; Esc closes it. The preview panel is 400 px with at least 720 px of content (superseded by R98: 480 px and always an overlay) and overlays below 1200 px.
- **C5** Seven top-bar actions: New, Assistant, Calendar, Conversations, Page guide, Notifications, Account. Recent lives in the palette and the flyout, not the bar.
- **C6** Roboto 11/12/13/14/16/20; interactive controls at least 28 px, 32 px preferred; Lucide outlines stand in for Font Awesome Light until the Kit is wired in.

## Rules R1–R125

- **R1** The global search is centred in the top bar (448 px at x = 496–944) on every page whatever the rail width, as the accepted shell top bar amendment (R9) requires; the brand zone and the actions zone are each 520 px, and a fixed 16 px gap separates the search from the status pill on its left and the New control on its right. (Zones are 496 / 448 / 496 since R99.)
- **R2** The vertical separator after the brand is removed; the bar reads brand · search · actions.
- **R3** Top-bar actions are one family of 36 px targets with 2 px gaps, left-aligned 16 px after the search, New's left edge on the right drawer's left edge (R99), with a flexible spacer before the account group; New is a 32 px white circle with a navy plus (tooltip New · N), the bar's only filled control; the Conversations and Notifications pills are the only other colour on the bar besides the logo.
- **R4** Rail glyphs are 22 px collapsed and 20 px expanded with 11 / 14 px labels; the WORKSPACES heading aligns with the glyph column (x = 30) instead of the panel edge.
- **R5** My Work carries one count pill (white, navy numeral) in both rail widths for the items due today; no other rail item carries a count, so there is one number to read. (Q2: the count is overdue plus due today.)
- **R6** The expanded rail lists workspaces only (with My Work, Favourites, More and Settings); a workspace's pages live in one place, the workspace menu, as a flyout or docked at 280 px. The rail never nests pages, so it never reflows and the current page is marked in one place.
- **R7** Flyout Create tiles drop from 60 to 48 px; the three link columns are 219 px; the hovered register row reveals a favourite star and a New chip; the current page carries the green bar.
- **R8** A More workspaces panel replaces an overflowing rail: every workspace as a 56 px tile with its page count and the current one marked, then Shared records and Administration columns, and Customise my rail in the footer. It opens from the rail More item with the same chrome as the flyout.
- **R9** The New menu follows the active workspace: Create in Sales first, then Shared records, then All record types.
- **R10** Notifications open as a 400 px popover: All · Unread · Mentions tabs, Mark all read, items with a tinted glyph, unread dot and relative time, and See all notifications.
- **R11** The Account menu names the working company (top bar amendment C1) and holds My account, Preferences, Notification settings, Keyboard shortcuts (?), Help centre and Sign out. (R104 adds Switch tenant and the last sign-in.)
- **R12** The global search field is the one input. Clicking it or pressing Ctrl K focuses it and it becomes the top row of a 448 px white card that starts at the field's own top edge (8 px corners, popover shadow, no scrim); the empty state shows Recent (with Clear), Go to page and Actions with Enter, Esc and prefix hints in the footer. There is no separate command palette.
- **R13** The right rail is a 48 px tab strip (36 px tiles, 18 px glyphs, counts on the tile corners) (open preview, Snapshot, Activity, Files, Conversations) shown on pages that have something to preview, registers and records; home and settings pages omit it, so their content runs to the screen edge (Q8 resolved). The sheets on this page stand for register pages and keep it.
- **R14** Overlays: the workspace flyout and the More panel are full-height drawers (760 × 852) flush to the rail, the top bar and the bottom edge, with a 1 px right edge and a shadow to the right and no scrim; their footer is pinned to the bottom so Show all and Edit menu never move. Dropdowns keep an 8 px radius; the search card follows the popover token (R44).
- **R15** Colour comes from 66 local PPO colour variables (30 core, 12 person, 24 workspace accent) (R59); translucent whites on navy are plain paints (6 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 28 / 40 / 55 / 72 / 85%) because a variable-bound paint drops its opacity in this environment.
- **R16** Badges: a white count pill with a navy numeral is the only badge on the bar, used for Conversations and Notifications alike; the red dot is reserved for system alerts and never carries a count.
- **R17** Every icon-only control has a tooltip 4 px below it: label plus shortcut where one exists (Search Ctrl K, New N, Assistant Ctrl J, Keyboard shortcuts ?); navy, 12 px white text; shown on hover and on keyboard focus.
- **R18** A status pill sits at the right end of the brand zone, left of the search, only while the state is not normal: Offline · n (warning; n = queued changes; click opens the queue and retries), Syncing (info), Saved (success, 2 s); the full sentence is the pill's tooltip. The normal state shows nothing.
- **R19** Below 1440 px the end zones shrink first (1280: 440 / 400 / 440, account as avatar only, tenant chip as initials). At 1024 the zones are 332 / 360 / 332, the search is 360 px and hides its Ctrl K chip, New stays the circle and the account shows the avatar only. Below 768 px the accepted phone header applies.
- **R20** Edge padding: 18 px to the logo on the left (centre at 40), 16 px after the account on the right; 16 px either side of the search; the search field border is white at 28%.
- **R21** Surfaces: navy is the primary chrome (top bar, rail); light grey (surface/chrome) is the secondary chrome (workspace flyout, docked menu, More panel, right rail); the page canvas is white. On grey chrome the hover is surface/chrome-hover, the current page is a white tile with the green bar, badges and Create tiles are white with a line/strong border and Find inputs are white. Dropdowns and the search results stay white because they are overlays over the page, not chrome. Record pages paint surface/workspace behind their cards.
- **R22** The collapsed rail is 80 px wide (the standard navigation-rail width); its glyph column is centred at x = 40 and the logo, the expanded rail (glyphs at x = 30, labels at x = 60) and the footer controls share it. Labels stay 11 px, which gives the longest name 11 px of side room.
- **R23** Inactive glyphs and labels are white at 84%; the active workspace and the current page are 100%.
- **R24** More uses the ellipsis glyph; expand and collapse use double chevrons, as the right rail does; dividers are inset 16 px in both widths; hover is surface/rail-hover, one tonal step above the rail and one below the active tile.
- **R25** A collapsed item shows a tooltip with the full workspace name to its right after 400 ms or at once on focus; the toggle's tooltip reads Expand navigation · Ctrl \ (Collapse navigation when open).
- **R26** When the workspace list is taller than the rail it scrolls beneath a 16 px fade above the fixed footer. (Drawn at 48 px on sheets AH and AN; R92, R113.)
- **R27** Clicking a workspace reopens the page last used there, not always its first page. Hovering a collapsed workspace may peek the first group of its workspace menu (Q3: kept as a 400 ms preview, off on touch).
- **R28** The docked workspace menu and the expanded rail are mutually exclusive: docking the menu collapses the rail to 80 px, and expanding the rail returns a docked menu to its flyout form. The collapsed strip still counts as docked. Navigation therefore never exceeds 360 px.
- **R29** The Find fields in the workspace flyout (240 px) and the More panel (284 px) start at x = 496, the left edge of the global search, so the three search fields line up when a panel is open.
- **R30** The docked menu collapses to a 12 px strip flush to the rail (surface/chrome, hairline right edge, a 4 × 40 px handle centred vertically) from its header Collapse button, by clicking its right edge, which lights up text/link blue on hover, or with Ctrl [. The strip's handle turns blue on hover with the tooltip Expand menu, and a click restores the 280 px menu; Unpin still returns it to the flyout.
- **R31** Panels and keyboard: Tab moves Find → Create tiles → links; arrow keys move within a group; typing anywhere in a panel focuses Find; Esc closes and returns focus to the rail item that opened it. Header buttons carry tooltips (Pin menu, Close · Esc).
- **R32** With a panel open, hovering another workspace on the rail swaps the panel's content after 150 ms without closing it; the header and Find placeholder follow.
- **R33** The star shown on a hovered link adds the page to Favourites, which feeds the Favourites rail page and the Favourites group; the Edit menu link (administrators only) sets the defaults for everyone.
- **R34** Badges on menu links count items waiting for the user (new leads, unread emails, handovers awaiting), never record totals; a zero shows no badge.
- **R35** While a panel is open its rail item shows the open tile (surface/rail-active without the green bar) and clicking it again closes the panel. Panel section headings are 11 px caps eyebrows; Recent lists record names first and never truncates; footers read All N pages › and Edit menu (administrators), or Customise my rail and Manage workspaces (administrators).
- **R36** Docked menu body: the groups scroll under the fixed header and Find field and above the fixed footer, with the 16 px fade (R26). All N pages expands the menu in place to every group (Reports, Imports, Settings) with collapsible eyebrow headings and a Show fewer return; the choice is remembered per workspace.
- **R37** Switching while docked: clicking another workspace on the rail switches the docked menu to that workspace and opens the page last used there (R27); hover never switches a docked menu (R32 applies to flyouts only).
- **R38** Below 1366 px the docked menu auto-collapses to the strip so registers keep at least 1000 px, and returns when the window grows; the person's own collapse choice is remembered separately.
- **R39** The docked menu opens with a FAVOURITES group of the person's starred pages for the workspace, shown only when it has entries; stars on hovered links add or remove them (R33). Find is 32 px with 13 px text and the footer link is 13 px, matching the flyout; Unpin and Collapse carry tooltips.
- **R40** Search behaviour: Ctrl K focuses the field from anywhere and toggles the card; filtering starts at two characters with a 150 ms debounce; arrows move, Enter opens, Ctrl Enter opens in a new tab; Esc clears the query first and closes on the second press; each group shows up to eight results with Show all; Clear on Recent empties the list.
- **R41** Typing shows a filter row with counts (All, Records, Pages, Actions, Help; zero counts muted). Prefixes narrow the scope as you type: > actions, / pages, ? help, # a reference such as SYN-QUO-000214.
- **R42** When one record clearly matches, a Best match card leads the results with its key facts and quick actions (Open, New activity, Call); matched text is bold in every result; an Ask the Assistant row closes the list for questions that are not lookups.
- **R43** States: a skeleton appears only if results take longer than 300 ms (Searching… in the footer); No results keeps the query editable and offers spelling, a reference example, Search everything including archived records, and the Assistant.
- **R44** Popover token for every menu that hangs from the top bar, including search: 8 px corners, shadow 0 8 24 at 18%, a 1 px line/soft edge, no scrim; menus sit 4 px below the bar and the search card starts at its field's top edge.
- **R45** The open drawer is a 432 px white preview panel plus the 48 px tab column, 480 px in all at 1440, so its left edge sits on the bar's zone line at x = 960, the right end of the global search; it overlays the content whenever the content would otherwise fall below 720 px (docked menu open, or below 1200 px). It is flush to the bar and the screen edge with square corners; only popovers are rounded (R44). The strip is the tab column in both states: the selected tab is a white tile with a 3 px green bar at the column edge, hover is surface/chrome-hover, and Files and Conversations show counts when non-zero. (R98: the drawer now overlays at every width.)
- **R46** The tab column stays visible while the drawer is open: it is the same strip in both states and holds tabs only. Any tab opens the drawer; the single Collapse control in the drawer header, the selected tab clicked again, Esc (when focused) or Ctrl ] close it. Behaviour: the preview follows the row clicked in a register (blue outline; the checkbox is separate, as the trial rules say); the last tab is remembered per workspace; the header's pop-out opens the full record; Esc closes the drawer when it has focus; the drawer never shows two collapse controls.
- **R47** Empty state: Nothing selected, with one line of guidance; the drawer never shows stale content from a previous page.
- **R48** Shortcut map: Ctrl \ expands or collapses the rail, Ctrl [ the left workspace menu, Ctrl ] the right drawer, Ctrl K the search, N new, Ctrl J the Assistant, ? the shortcut sheet. (Shift F enters focus mode, R84. Q16: letters are proposals until the app's key handling is audited.)
- **R49** Bar popovers and keyboard: arrows move, Enter activates, Esc closes and returns focus to the button that opened it; typing matches item labels; the New menu's letter accelerators (D deal, L lead, A activity, Q quotation, C contact, O organisation, S site) create at once. Every icon-only control in a popover has a tooltip.
- **R50** One popover is open at a time: opening another closes the first; clicking outside closes; the button shows the open tile while its popover is open; popovers never dim the page.
- **R51** Unread and mention markers are link blue; red is reserved for system alerts (extends R16). Notifications are grouped under TODAY and YESTERDAY, then by date; today's items show relative times, older ones the clock time; hovering a row reveals Mark read and Open; people show an initials avatar, system events a tinted glyph.
- **R52** Brand block: a 32 px mark centred on the rail's glyph column (x = 40), then the wordmark set in two weights (Powerplants in Medium, One in Regular at 72% white, tracking −1%). The block is the home control with the bar's hover tile, focus ring and the tooltip Home · My Work. No separator, tagline or company name; the company is named in the account menu (C1).
- **R53** Superseded by the tenant switcher (8 November 2026): the DEMO environment tag is replaced by the tenant switcher, in which Demo company is a tenant (R72, R100, R104).
- **R57** Surface roles: canvas white; chrome #f4f5f7 for the flyout, docked menu, More panel, right rail and drawer column; hover #f0f2f5 on white and #e8ebf0 on chrome; navy-tint #e9eef5 for selection, avatars and open controls; line/soft on white, line/strong on chrome, line/control for inputs. No two greys share a role.
- **R58** Two greens: brand/green (#62bb46, the logo green) marks the active workspace on the navy rail; brand/green-deep (#3d9a37) marks the current page, selected tab and current tile on light surfaces, where it measures 3.6:1 against white. Green is never a fill behind text, and the status success colour is a separate dark green.
- **R59** Tokens: 29 core variables after consolidating surface/2 into surface/hover, status/neutral-surface into surface/navy-tint, state/focus into text/link and surface/selected into white tiles, and retiring rail-head and hairline (surface/rail was added later, R105, making 30 core). Every text pairing on the sheets measures at least 4.5:1; translucent whites on navy are 84% (inactive), 72% (secondary), 55% (headings) and 10–28% (tiles and borders); disabled chip text is 70% secondary.
- **R60** Top bar utilities (Assistant, Calendar, Conversations, Page guide, Notifications) sit centred between New and the account divider: twin fill spacers share the free width (6 px minimum at 1440 and 1280, 0 at 1024 where the zone is full), so at narrower widths the cluster closes up toward New instead of breaking.
- **R61** No scrim behind the desktop navigation drawers (C, E, M). They are non-modal, close on outside click or Esc, and the 4 / 0 / 24 side shadow carries the separation from the white canvas; a wash would match the chrome grey and blur the drawer edge. A scrim is reserved for modal dialogs and for the phone shell, where a drawer covers the content.
- **R62** Popovers anchor to the edge of their trigger nearest the screen edge, 4 px below the bar: New and the tenant switcher left-aligned, Notifications and Account right-aligned. The global search drops from its own field.
- **R63** The docked menu and the workspace flyout list the same registers, handovers and insights for a workspace; only the Create tiles and the Recent list are flyout-only.
- **R64** More panel tiles carry no chevron; the check marks the current workspace, the same pattern as the tenant switcher, and titles such as "Estimating & quotation" fit without truncation.
- **R65** Secondary lines in menus stay on one line (tenant switcher, notification meta); copy is shortened before it wraps.
- **R66** The right rail strip hosts six tabs: four record tabs (Snapshot, now the eye glyph, Activity, Files, Conversations), a divider, then two shell helpers (Assistant, Page guide). One panel is open at a time; the top-bar sparkle and info buttons are shortcuts to the same two tabs.
- **R67** Assistant panel (sheet O): 432 px panel in the drawer with a context chip for the current page, answers that cite their sources and offer action chips, and a composer with voice and send. Carl answers and drafts only; the person reviews and sends. Ctrl J toggles it.
- **R68** Page guide (sheet Q): the DK-07 guide for the current page in the drawer: purpose, key actions with their shortcuts, fields and rules, related guides, and a feedback row; Open Help Centre sits in the header.
- **R69** Conversations peek (sheet P): the Notifications card pattern, right-aligned to its icon, with threads, unread and mention tabs and a link to Conversations. Calendar carries no badge and navigates straight to Calendar.
- **R70** Dialogs (sheets R, V): 12 px radius, 0 / 16 / 48 shadow at 24 %, a 32 % navy scrim over the whole window, focus trapped, Esc closes, the primary action at the right of the footer. The scrim appears nowhere else on desktop (R61).
- **R71** Banners sit at the top of the content region, 40 px, one at a time, highest severity first, never over the rail or the bar. Toasts sit bottom-left of the content region, 24 px inset, 360 wide, newest at the bottom, at most three, dismissed after 6 s except Danger, Esc dismisses the newest, and each is announced through a polite live region (sheet S, four tones each).
- **R72** Demo tenant (sheet T): the chip turns amber with navy text, a 3 px amber stripe runs under the bar on every page, the tooltip says "synthetic data only" and the window title ends "· Demo". Demo and live data are never shown the same way.
- **R73** Offline queue (sheet U): the status pill opens a 360 px queue left-aligned to the pill: queued, needs-review and waiting-for-Wi-Fi rows, Retry now, and a footer that says changes sync on reconnect.
- **R74** Customise my rail (sheet V): a dialog from the More panel. Drag to reorder, add or remove workspaces, a labels switch and Reset to default; changes apply on Save.
- **R75** 1024 × 768 (sheet W): bar zones 332 / 360 / 332; the docked menu collapses to its 12 px strip and opens as an overlay; the right drawer overlays the content with its left shadow as it does at every width (R98); flyouts behave as at 1440.
- **R76** 1920: zones 720 / 480 / 720, the search grows to 480 and stays centred; above 1920 the zones stop growing and the bar centres in the window (Components reference bar).
- **R77** The expanded rail and the docked menu are never open together, since both would take 520 px of chrome: opening one collapses the other to its strip or icon width.
- **R78** Badges cap at 99+. Expanded-rail labels, the tenant chip and the account name truncate with an ellipsis on one line; the chip shows a short tenant name of at most 14 characters.
- **R79** Elevation: bar and rails flat; popovers, peeks and toasts 0 / 8 / 24 at 18 %; flyouts and drawers 4 / 0 / 24 at 18 % toward the content; dialogs 0 / 16 / 48 at 24 % over the scrim. Motion: flyouts and drawers 160 ms ease-out, popovers 120 ms, tooltips after 400 ms of hover, toasts 200 ms; reduced motion turns all of them into fades.
- **R80** Content region: 24 px gutters, no maximum width; the window title reads "Deals · Sales · Powerplants One".
- **R81** Incoming call (sheet X): a 360 px card bottom-right of the content region, persistent until answered or declined; the caller is matched to a contact and shown with organisation, role and live context chips; Answer opens the contact and starts the call log. During a call the status slot shows a success pill with the name and timer (pill set, State=On call); clicking it returns to the call card.
- **R82** First paint (sheet Y): the shell chrome renders at once from cached state; the content region shows a page skeleton of surface/chrome blocks (header, filters, table) until data arrives and is replaced progressively; no shell-level spinner. Reconnection is the pill cycle Offline → Syncing → Saved; an expired session shows the Danger banner and then the system sign-in page.
- **R83** Sparse rail (sheet Z): the rail lists only the workspaces the person may open, in the standard order with the standard labels; the first visible workspace takes the current state on sign-in; More stays while Knowledge or the Assistant remain available and hides only when nothing is left.
- **R84** Focus mode (sheet AA): Shift F or the page header's expand control hides the rail and the right strip, keeps the bar and shows the exit pill top-left of the content region; Esc or the pill exits; the state is remembered per page for the session; search, popovers and the New menu keep working.
- **R85** Focus order (sheet AB): skip link first, then the bar left to right, the rail, the main content and the right strip; grouped controls are one tab stop with arrow keys inside; the focus ring is 2 px text/link with a 2 px offset, white on navy; layers trap focus and return it to their trigger; Esc closes the newest layer; toasts never take focus.
- **R86** Amber numbered markers and legend cards are design-file annotations only and never ship.
- **R87** Tablet (sheet AC): below 1024 the docked menu never docks and overlays replace pushes; hover has no meaning, so tooltips, hover peek and hover rows are off; every control keeps a 44 × 44 px hit area; the bar keeps New, Assistant, Conversations, Notifications and the account while Calendar sits on the rail and the Page guide in the strip; the search narrows to 230–262 px; below 768 the accepted phone header applies.
- **R88** Bulk selection (sheet AD): a navy floating bar bottom-centre of the content region while rows are selected, with the count, the register's actions and Clear; toasts stay bottom-left and the call card bottom-right; when the width cannot hold them the toasts move above the bar; Delete confirms through the Dialog pattern.
- **R89** Page states (sheet AE): a page that fails or is not permitted shows the shell-owned Page state centred in the content region while bar, rail and strip keep working, with Try again or Go to My Work and an error reference; the full-page system pages remain for the cases where the shell itself cannot load.
- **R90** Dialog set (sheet AF): Discard changes, Session renewal and Switch tenant follow the dialog pattern with the safe choice first and the destructive action in status/danger; session renewal keeps unsaved work on the page.
- **R91** New user (sheet AG): first sign-in shows no counts, the Notifications card's empty state, no Favourites group in the flyout and no Recent in search; the rail is the default set for the person's role.
- **R92** Stress (sheet AH): proves R78 visually: counts cap at 99+, the tenant chip truncates at 96 px, the account name at 120 px and collapsed-rail labels at 72 px; twelve workspaces scroll behind a 48 px fade with the footer fixed.
- **R93** States in the Components section: the navigation progress line (2 px, appears after 300 ms, ends at first paint), the File drop overlay (record pages only, drops go to the Files tab) and the search actions mode (">" lists actions, "/" pages, "?" help).
- **R94** Phone alignment: the accepted phone header absorbs from this shell the tenant switcher (account sheet), Assistant and Page guide (the phone More sheet), the status pill (header), banners (under the header), toasts (above the bottom bar) and dialogs (full-width sheets); nothing else from the desktop shell applies below 768.
- **R95** Below 1024 the workspace flyout and the More panel are capped at the viewport width minus the rail (754 px at 834) and their three-column groups collapse to two columns under about 700 px; the 760 px layout applies from 1024 upward.
- **R96** Layers never create browser history: flyouts, peeks, popovers, drawers, dialogs and the search card open and close without an entry, so Back always navigates pages and never has to close a layer first; Esc and outside clicks remain the only ways to dismiss.
- **R97** The window title carries the unread count ahead of the page, as in "(3) Deals · Sales · Powerplants One", and clears it when the count is zero; links that leave Powerplants One (MYOB, SharePoint, supplier sites) open in a new tab and show the external-link glyph already used in the drawer headers.
- **R98** The right drawer overlays the content at every width (Dean, 8 November 2026: registers and tables must never be condensed to make room). It floats over the right 480 px of the content region with the 4 / 0 / 24 shadow toward the content, its tab column standing where the strip stands, with no scrim; the content beside it stays live, registers keep their column widths and scroll sideways if needed, and Esc, the collapse control or a strip tab closes it. Sheets M, O, Q and W show it.
- **R99** At 1440 New's left edge sits on the right drawer's left edge (x 960) and the search fills the room between the status pill and New: zones 496 / 448 / 496, the field at x 496–944, 16 px gaps either side, the pill's right edge at 480 and the actions zone's 16 px padding kept. The search card, the flyout and More panel Find fields (240 and 284 px) and the New menu, Offline queue and tooltips all follow. At 1280 and below the drawer edge falls elsewhere and the narrower zones stand.
- **R100** The tenant chip is a field, not a button: at rest it carries the search field's 12 % white fill inside the constant 28 % border, 16 % on hover and 24 % while the switcher is open, with the chevron turned. The demo tenant keeps its amber fill (R72). The same fills apply on every detached bar.
- **R101** People get a colour: six muted pairs (sage, sky, lilac, sand, rose, teal; person/*-surface and -ink, all 5:1 or better) assigned deterministically per person and used on every avatar in the shell: the account block and menu, Conversations, Notifications, the call card and the tenant list of people. Tenants keep navy and the demo amber; the Assistant keeps its navy and green mark.
- **R102** Workspaces get an accent: twelve pairs (accent/*-surface and -ink, 3:1 or better) used only as a 28 px chip behind the workspace icon in the More panel tiles and the flyout and docked headers.
