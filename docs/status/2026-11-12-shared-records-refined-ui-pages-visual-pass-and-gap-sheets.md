The visual pass that the [proposal record](../decisions/shared-records-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-SHARED-RECORDS-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) recommended on 11 November 2026 is done. The 101 sheets that had been checked by script only were rendered at full size and reviewed sheet by sheet. The scripted checks had passed, but the renders showed defects on about half of those sheets: the More item of the rail clipped on fifteen tablet sheets; the settings column collapsed to two letters in every Administration editor; fact, check and context strips truncated at 1440; text running out of cards; a few counts that contradicted their own sheet; docked-menu and view-tile selections that did not match the open view; and confirming buttons drawn as secondary in 32 dialogs and panels. These were repaired in the file, and the record lists them as V1 to V10. The fourteen dialogs and panels the first audit listed as gaps were then drawn: Add number, Flag number, Add site party, Link equipment, End a link, Review evidence, a desktop Record history form, Record support evidence, and Filters, Columns, Export, Export for a restricted reader, Save view and Address lookup. The file now holds 198 sheets on 15 pages (76 desktop, 46 dialogs and panels, 31 states, 16 tablet, 29 phone); no earlier sheet changed its node ID. The record's inventory, assumptions (A17 to A19), verification, gaps and polish sections are updated, and the file's Read me index and audit cards carry the same.

It remains a **proposal**. Still open: the three conflicts inside the brief that need Dean's decision (survey state counts, a backup's stage dates, one weekday); the control-border refinement in the shared kit; seven polish items the pass found and did not fix (for example the page body behind a side panel is not drawn on four sheets, and two state sheets promise content they do not show); tablet sheets for Test instruments and Administration; dark mode, 200 % reflow and prototype links; Dean's acceptance and the named version of the file; the design register and page guidance, which follow in their own change once the design is accepted.

Checked: every sheet has now been rendered and inspected at least once. After the repairs, 15 sheets (eight repaired, seven new) were rendered again and inspected; the other repaired sheets were re-checked by script only (structure, text wider than its frame, truncation in strips and small tables, content taller than its sheet), and a script cannot judge whether a wrapped strip or table reads well. The structural audit over all 15 pages reports nothing loose, no overlap, no child outside its sheet and one uniquely coded label per sheet. Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check`, `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py` and `python3 scripts/check_naming.py`. No application code, route, migration, permission, integration or deployment is created or authorised. Every record, person, value and date shown is synthetic.
