---
title: Documents and messages refined UI pages proposed in Figma
date: 2026-11-12
---

Dean asked for the refined UI pages of Documents and messages (the Document library, Outputs and Templates pages reached from More › Shared records, and the Email, Calendar and Customer messages pages of the Email workspace) to be built in the new private Figma file "PPO - Documents & messages module", inside the accepted shell and to at least the standard of the Sales module file, so that the pages where PPO's evidence leaves a module have one consistent, reviewable design set before any page is built further. The [proposal record](../decisions/documents-and-messages-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-DOCS-MESSAGES-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) claims the work while the build is in progress. It holds the sources, the nine-field conformance declaration with the ownership of each scope, the rules applied, assumptions and proposed departures A1 to A21 (among them the 12 px collapsed strip as the accepted shell draws it, the two proposed docked menus and the More panel's My workspace column, the proposed output families, customer message classes M5 to M10 and customer email template family, Melbourne time and the story reconciliations of Addendum A3.10), what is not drawn, and the open questions. Drawn so far: the page template, the local copies of the shell, kit, icons and chrome under the accepted component names, and the first Outputs sheets (the register, an issued job pack record and the Request issue dialog); the six scope pages, the interaction states and the administration pages are being drawn. The brief's questions Q2 to Q10 were answered by their stated defaults under Dean's standing instruction and can be reversed. The design is a proposal: Dean has not accepted it and no named version is saved yet. The file's link is not recorded here (ADR-0050, ADR-0053).

Checked: the copied shell components were compared with their sources by node count (equal) and by render; the first Outputs sheets were rendered and read and pass the structural pass. Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`. Not done yet: the rest of the build, the sheet inventory with node IDs, the position audit, the evidence, counts, story, capability and authority scans, the contrast table and the audit. Not in scope: prototype links, a person's keyboard, screen reader, zoom and device review, the design register and per-route page guidance (a separate reviewed change), and any application code, route, migration, permission or integration with SharePoint, Microsoft 365, Graph, a mail or SMS provider or a customer. Every record, person, hash, message and mailbox shown is synthetic.
