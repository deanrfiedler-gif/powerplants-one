title: Reporting module refined UI pages proposed in Figma
date: 2026-11-10
---

Dean asked for the complete, refined UI page set of the Reporting workspace to be designed in a new private Figma file, "PPO - Reporting module (proposal)", inside the accepted shell and to at least the standard of the Sales module file, so that Reporting (RP-01 to RP-06) has one consistent, reviewable design before any page is built. The [proposal record](../decisions/reporting-module-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-REPORTING-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) lists 221 sheets and cards with their Figma node IDs on 11 scope pages: Reports overview, Dashboards, the four department report pages, Report definitions, Report builder, Saved views and schedules, Interaction states and Administration. Every figure carries its rule, counts, source, as-at time, completeness and definition version; a missing value is never zero, a pending definition shows no value, and no target, threshold, score, average or good-or-bad colour appears. During the build Dean asked for more professional charts and for people to build their own reports and dashboards. The record adds a chart vocabulary (column charts by month, segmented bars, ranked bars, stage steps, headline numbers, hover tooltips), Dashboards and a governed Report builder. It also explains why goal lines, averages, pies, forecasts stacked with won value and public links stay out under the accepted rules.

Checked: every sheet was rendered and inspected by its builder. A scripted pass over all 11 scope pages found nothing outside its bounds, no overlap, no wrapped register cell and Roboto as the only font. That pass recomputed 374 rates from their counts and checked 2,753 dated weekdays, with no mismatch in either. Forbidden words appear only inside the rules that forbid them. The chart palette was validated for colour-vision separation, and its low-contrast slots carry printed values and a table view. `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py` pass. No application code, route, migration, permission, scheduler or integration changed; acceptance and a named version are Dean's.
