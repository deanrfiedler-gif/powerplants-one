Dean requested the final visual pass on dialogs, phone typography and spacing, and Sales/Service consistency. The existing private Figma trial now shares dialog title/body typography, divided right-aligned footers and 44 px actions; phone gutters, card padding and text hierarchy; and distinct warning, information and success status treatments. Existing buttons and compact deal-row components were refined in place. This continues `codex/shell-concept-prototype-record` and its existing draft review.

Final screenshots cover the four desktop dialogs, both phone deal lists, short-phone records, Service statuses and confirmation, and record actions. Cached status-colour rendering and a small conflict-footer overflow found during review were corrected and recaptured. Source checks retain 66 reachable screens and 461 destination actions, with no dangling target, horizontal phone-text overflow or undersized phone action. Six sampled token pairs measure at least 5.33:1; this is not a full accessibility audit. Text, component instances and the existing logo assets remain editable source content.

The [decision record](../decisions/shell-concept-prototype.md), [working shell master](../design/development/systems/shell.md) and [component consumer notes](../design/development/component-bindings/2026-11-05-shell-concept-prototype.md) record the final visual rules and review scope. The represented layouts are ready for owner visual approval. Complete shared-shell sign-off still requires state continuity, full phone scope and reconciliation with accepted shell rules, plus real-device and accessibility proof. No application, deployment, business authority, acceptance fingerprint or public artwork changed.
