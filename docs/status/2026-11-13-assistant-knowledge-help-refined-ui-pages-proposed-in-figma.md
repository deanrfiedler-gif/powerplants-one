Dean asked for the refined UI pages of the three shared helpers — the Assistant (Carl, in the shell's right drawer, with answer evidence, proposal review, drafting and domain help, voice capture and recurring checks), Knowledge (search, article detail, authoring, review, publication and learning intake) and Help (the Help Centre and the page guide every page carries) — to be built in the new private Figma file "PPO - Assistant, Knowledge & Help" inside the accepted shell, so that the helpers have one consistent, reviewable design set before any of their pages is built. The file now holds 304 sheets on 11 scope pages (AI-01, AI-03, AI-02, AI-04, AI-05, AI-07, DK-04, DK-05, DK-07 with `system:guidance`, the cross-cutting interaction states, and administration), each with a Flows and notes card, plus Read me cards for the sheet index with node IDs, the assumptions, departures and open questions, and the audit with a recommended order. The [proposal record](../decisions/assistant-knowledge-help-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-ASSIST-KNOWLEDGE-HELP-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) lists every sheet with its node ID and declares conformance per scope. It also corrects the guide coverage reading at bdcdd347: with `docs/guides/*.md` counted, there are 369 Draft guides, 21 In review and 3 entries with no guide, against the brief's 367 all Draft. Design acceptance is Dean's and has not been given; no named version is saved yet.

Checked: scripted structural pass, position audit, authority scan (330 button and menu labels), evidence, date and story scans over every sheet; all 30 brief scenarios mapped to sheets; contrast computed from the PPO colour variables, with all 19 pairs passing; renders inspected per sheet, except the few listed in the record's section 6. `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py` were run for this change. No application code, route, migration, permission, provider, speech service or scheduler is created; the provider stays Simulated, and all data is synthetic.
