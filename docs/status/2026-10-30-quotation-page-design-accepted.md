date: 2026-10-30

Dean accepted the Figma design for the customer quotation page and the staff Customer views display. Asked "Do you accept the design with the recommended answers to D1–D15 and Q1–Q8?", he answered "Accept and lock in". The [design decision](../decisions/quotation-page-customer-views-figma-design.md) (PPO-ES05-QUOTE-PAGE-FIGMA-DEC) records departures D1–D15 and the answers to Q1–Q8: a page at an indicative `/quote/[token]`, outside the application shell, showing the exact issued document in a plain Powerplants frame with no Accept or Sign; notices that show no prices when a link is withdrawn, replaced or closed; a validity-ended banner; a Send quotation composer on the release page's Distribution step, with the PDF switch and its warning; the Customer views line on the deal, with a phone sheet; a Withdraw link confirmation; and the My Work follow-up suggestion. The privacy gate is shown honestly: tracking reads "Not tracked" for every real recipient until Dean confirms the privacy notice. Twenty-nine icon-free captures with hashes are retained in [quotation-page-design-r01](../reference/ui/estimating/quotation-page-design-r01/README.md), and the `scope:ES-05` register entry and its design page cite them. In Figma the page is `2221:85`, the capture copies sit on the Captures page from y = 470,000, the File index row is `2771:404435`, and the new parts moved to Components.

Checked: on 23 October a scripted layout and wording check found no overlaps, nothing outside its section, only Roboto fonts, bound text colours, no Accept or Sign control, and no view that names a person; 334 icons in the capture copies were swapped to the placeholder, with none left. Claude saved the named Figma version "Quotation page & customer views — accepted" in the desktop app on 31 October 2026, when Dean answered "Yes, you save it", and saw it at the top of version history. Not done: the prototype was not played in Present mode. Nothing is built: QV-S1 to QV-S3 and ADR-0059 keep their own gates. The Deal record, My Work and Quotation detail pages and captures are not edited, and the Quotation release page `2244:50245`, accepted separately in PR #757, copies the composer unchanged. No application code, route, migration, permission or parent ID changes.
