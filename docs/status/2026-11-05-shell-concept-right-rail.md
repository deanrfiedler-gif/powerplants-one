---
title: Expanded right rail examples for Sales and Service
date: 2026-11-05
---

The private shell concept now has two desktop examples of the right rail expanding into a record panel. Both offer Snapshot, Activity, Files and Conversations. The Sales example follows a synthetic Sunline deal. The Service example follows the authorised Riverbend work order and its pending visit.

The ten example states and 73 prototype actions passed source checks for destinations, tab fit, selected record and return targets. The [design study](../decisions/shell-concept-prototype.md#expanded-right-rail-examples) records the proposed contents, examples and implementation limits. The [shell master](../design/development/systems/shell.md) and its consumer binding identify the study for review.
