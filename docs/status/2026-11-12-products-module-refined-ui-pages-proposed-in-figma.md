Dean asked for the complete, refined UI page set of Products (PD-01 to PD-05: the catalogue and product record, publication, pricing, compatibility and import) to be designed in a new private Figma file, "PPO - Products module", inside the accepted shell and to at least the standard of the Sales module file. All five Products pages already have owner-accepted designs that the native build of 7 October 2026 has not reached, so the file redraws those designs in the refined shell as the target and marks every sheet where the build or its data contract differs with a Build difference card. The [proposal record](../decisions/products-module-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-PRODUCTS-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) lists 122 sheets and 7 notes cards with their node IDs on seven scope pages: Catalogue and product record (34), Publication (21), Pricing (19), Compatibility (20), Import (19), Interaction states (3) and Administration (6). It holds the conformance declaration for each scope, the rules applied, 24 file-wide assumptions plus each page's own, and the build differences as an ordered list of 15 UI changes and 17 contract changes for a later, separately authorised build step. The flagship journeys are the DP-25 day (submitted, independently reviewed and published once), the ClimaLogic CX2 replacement chain handed to Equipment, and the contactor substitution handed to Engineering.

Checked: a scripted pass over every scope page found nothing outside its bounds, no overlap, every sheet labelled and Roboto as the only font, after six phone sheets were corrected. An evidence scan over 17,371 text nodes found no earlier fixture, ISO date, gendered pronoun or forbidden word outside a quoted rule. The 20 contrast pairs computed from the colour variables pass. All 30 scenarios of the prompt have a sheet. `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py` pass. No application code, route, migration, permission or integration changed, and no Figma link or file key is recorded; acceptance and a named version are Dean's.
