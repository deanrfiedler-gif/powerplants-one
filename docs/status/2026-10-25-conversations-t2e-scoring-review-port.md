PR #686 conflicted with main because PR #680 had merged a different, reviewed T2E-SYN-01 corpus definition into the same catalogue and documents. On 25 October 2026 Dean decided: "Proceed with the recommendation: treat #686 as superseded, port its distinct scoring/gold fixes and review-document verification onto #680's tooling in a new PR, and leave #686 open for me to close."

Draft PR #708 carries out that port and keeps #680's recipes, materialised corpus and commands. Catalogue schema 3 corrects the L03 and L11 oracle wording. It also declares each case's scoring branch and marks application invariants, which are excluded from model-quality denominators. The tools now generate case cards and a 117-sample table beside each proposed inventory and refuse changed or missing review documents. Re-recording now also accepts these reviewer annotations, provided every corpus fact and gold support binding is identical; the retained #680 corpus was re-recorded that way. The [plan](../decisions/conversations-t2e-evaluation.md#scoring-and-review-document-port--25-october-2026) and [evidence](../testing/evidence/conversations-t2e/README.md#scoring-and-review-document-port--25-october-2026) record the details.

#686 was closed on 26 October 2026 as superseded; its corpus changes, tooling and retained artifacts are not imported. ChatGPT's independent review on 26 October found that re-recording accepted any claim wording change and that the case scoring policy was not enforced. Both are corrected: claim and assessment changes are limited to the port's exact listed corrections, and scoring is fixed by case ID in validation and re-recording ([evidence](../testing/evidence/conversations-t2e/README.md#independent-review-corrections--26-october-2026)). The corrected tooling has not yet re-recorded the retained corpus, which is on Dean's workstation. No corpus approval, calibration, live request, spending or merge authority is granted, and the retained naming limitation still awaits Dean's decision.

On 30 October 2026 Codex re-reviewed head `3df40f4a` and found both corrections addressed, with no defects. Under Dean's standing order of 30 October, Codex then decided to adopt the narrowed re-recording rule for this port: only the exact listed gold corrections, and scoring fixed by case ID. This settles the plan's earlier "for Dean's review" hold on that rule; the [decision chapter](../decisions/conversations-t2e-evaluation.md#narrowed-rule-adopted-under-the-standing-order--30-october-2026) records it. It is an agent decision, not Dean's personal acceptance. It does not approve the corpus, settle the naming limitation, give access to the retained workstation, or authorise calibration, live evaluation or spending. To reverse it, amend the rule in a separately reviewed PR, or revert the tooling through a normal PR, keeping the retained corpus files and provenance.
