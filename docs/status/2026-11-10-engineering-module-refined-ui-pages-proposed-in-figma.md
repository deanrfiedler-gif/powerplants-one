title: Engineering module refined UI pages proposed in Figma
date: 2026-11-10
---

Dean asked for the refined UI pages of the Engineering module to be built in a new private Figma file, "PPO - Engineering module (proposal)", inside the accepted shell ("PPO - Shell", named version "Shell refined concept — accepted", 8 November 2026) and to the standard of the accepted Sales module file, so that Engineering has one consistent, reviewable design set before the EN-02 to EN-08 pages are redrawn in code. The [proposal record](../decisions/engineering-module-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-ENGINEERING-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) lists the 193 sheets with their Figma node IDs on 12 scope pages: Engineering workload and the proposed Engineering Pulse, the Engineering package record, Design basis and interfaces, Drawings and controlled documents, Technical queries and submittals, Technical reviews and issues, Materials and substitutions, Change review, Commissioning and as-built, the Engineering extract of Insights, Interaction states and Administration. Each page carries desktop sheets at 1440, dialogs and panels, states, tablet at 1024, phone at 390 and 320, and a notes card with its conformance declaration. The record holds the nine-field declaration with the ownership of each scope (native CAD authors, SharePoint stores, PPO controls; review, authorise and issue by independent people; EN-05 owns review and issue, EN-06 the materials release and Supply handover, EN-07 the technical decision while the project manager owns implementation authority, EN-08 the test basis and as-built release while Service, Equipment and Projects own their acceptance, and the Workshop owns test definitions), the rules applied, assumptions and proposed departures A1 to A23, the audit with its corrections, refinements, gaps, feature candidates and polish items in a recommended order, and the open questions. The eight questions in Dean's brief were answered by their stated defaults under his standing order and can be reversed. The design is a proposal: Dean has not accepted it and no named version is saved yet. The file's link is not recorded here (ADR-0050, ADR-0053).

Checked: sheets were rendered and read during each batch; a structural pass and a position audit over all 12 scope pages found 193 sheets, each inside its section with one matching label above it, no overlap at any level, no wrapped text in a register cell, Roboto as the only font family, and empty separator and Scratch pages; scans found no cost or effort figure and "Open in SharePoint" only as a link; the contrast of every text pair in use was computed from the PPO colour variables, and one pair, white on brand/green-deep at 3.57:1 on the record toolbar primary, is recorded as audit item C1 for the Shell file. About 360 texts write dates without the year, as the accepted Sales sheets do; this is recorded as audit item R1 rather than changed. Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`. Not done: a file-wide text comparison of every reference and value against the dataset as one pass, prototype links, a person's keyboard, screen reader, zoom and device review, the design register and per-route page guidance (a separate reviewed change), and any application code, route, migration, permission or integration. Every record, person, value and date shown is synthetic.
