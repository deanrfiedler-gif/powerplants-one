Dean accepted the Figma design for DK-05, knowledge authoring, review and learning intake. Asked "Do you accept the DK-05 design with the recommended answers to Q1–Q7?", he answered: "Accept and lock in". The [design decision](../decisions/knowledge-authoring-figma-design.md) (PPO-DK05-KNOWLEDGE-AUTHORING-FIGMA-DEC) records departures D1–D12 and answers Q1–Q7:
- one worklist for learning items and articles, by state, owner and product family;
- four triage outcomes, with the source link always kept;
- a one-page editor with safety notes as their own reviewed block and five-dimension applicability with Unknown allowed;
- review independent of the author, and validation of a hypothesis only with evidence;
- publishing an exact revision, successors for later edits, withdrawal that keeps history, and Publication not confirmed with a safe retry;
- phone capture and approval.

Twenty-one icon-free captures with hashes are retained in [knowledge-authoring-design-r01](../reference/ui/knowledge-authoring/knowledge-authoring-design-r01/README.md), and the design register page for `scope:DK-05` cites them. In Figma the page sits after Knowledge · DK-04, the capture copies sit on the Captures page from y = 480,000, and the seven new parts moved to Components.

Checked:
- No Font Awesome instance, stray vector or prototype link is left in the capture copies.
- Roboto only, one selected rail item and the Conversations action on every desktop and tablet top bar.

Not done:

No application code, route, migration, permission, grant, publication or parent ID changes.
