Dean accepted the Figma design for the CS-04 site register and site workspace (`/sites`, `/sites/[id]`): "I accept the Sites CS-04 design with your recommendations, including D1–D6 and Q1–Q3. Then lock in all seven accepted pages." The [decision](../decisions/sites-figma-design.md) (PPO-CS04-SITES-FIGMA-DEC) records departures D1–D6 and the recommended answers to Q1–Q3. The [retained captures](../reference/ui/sites/sites-design-r01/README.md) (PPO-CS04-SITES-FIGMA-R01) are eight icon-free PNGs with hashes, covering desktop, tablet, phone 390 and 320, and three phone states. The scope-cs-04, route-sites and route-sites-id page notes gain "Accepted design" paragraphs.
- **Register:** a grid of sites with readiness, biosecurity, areas, equipment and next visit, opened from More, with site views in the side menu.
- **Site workspace:** the accepted record layout with a "Before the next visit" bar, a readiness card, open work and an equipment table that keeps "Installed in" and "Serves" apart. Readiness is never confirmed on this page; it links to Access & readiness (CS-06).

The named Figma version "Sites — accepted" was saved on 18 October 2026 at Dean's request. The File index row and page header were updated after acceptance. The proposed top-bar Conversations action stays part of a separate, still-open shell amendment. The page is not built in this form and building it is not decided. Customer site-readiness confirmation (field readiness gate P4) is not built, and where it is recorded is settled with P4. The images are design compositions with synthetic data, not application captures. No code, route, migration, permission, capability or parent ID changes.
