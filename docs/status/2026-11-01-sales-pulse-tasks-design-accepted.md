title: Sales Pulse and Tasks design accepted
date: 2026-11-01

Dean accepted the Figma design for Sales Pulse and Tasks. Asked "Do you accept the Pulse and Tasks proposal with its recommended answers?", he answered: "Accept, lock in". The [design decision](../decisions/sales-pulse-tasks-figma-design.md) (PPO-SH02-SALES-PULSE-TASKS-FIGMA-DEC) records D1–D28 and the answers to Q1–Q12: Deals stays the Sales landing page; Tasks keeps its rail item as a filtered view; Calls to log appears on Pulse, your own calls only; Owner: Everyone needs `activity.edit`; and value and expected close are to join Deal attention. Thirty icon-free captures are retained in [design r01](../reference/ui/crm/sales-pulse-tasks-design-r01/README.md). The Purpose text in both routes' design pages and draft guides is corrected, because it described a Board, a List and My Work views these routes do not have.

Checked: no Font Awesome instance is left in the capture copies, the temporary capture page is deleted, and every link on the Figma page leads to a frame on it. Dean saved the named Figma version "Sales Pulse & Tasks — accepted" on 1 November 2026. Not done: the flows were not played in Present mode; value and expected close are not yet drawn; and the built pages are not changed. No application code, route, migration, capability, grant or parent ID changes.
