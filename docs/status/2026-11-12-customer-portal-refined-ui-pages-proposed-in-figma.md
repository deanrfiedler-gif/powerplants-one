title: Customer portal refined UI pages proposed in Figma
date: 2026-11-12
---

Dean asked for the refined UI pages of the Customer portal to be built in the new private Figma file "PPO - Customer portal module", so that the portal has one consistent, reviewable design set on both of its sides before any page is built: the customer portal as a customer sees it (CP-01 to CP-06, in the accepted customer portal shell, phone first) and the staff Customer portal workspace (in the accepted staff shell, reached through More). The [proposal record](../decisions/customer-portal-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-PORTAL-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) holds the sources, the nine-field conformance declaration with the ownership of each scope, the inventory of 184 sheets with node IDs (143 customer sheets, 40 staff sheets and one specification sheet for both), the rules applied, the ten questions answered by their stated defaults under Dean's standing instruction, assumptions and proposed departures A1 to A30, what is not drawn, and the audit with its recommended order. It is the first repository record of the CP-03 Projects and CP-04 Visits and reports portal pages, which until now existed only as proposals in the design file. Three things are raised for Dean to decide: the weekday in the dataset for quotation SYN-PPO-QUO-000214 does not match the 2026 calendar; the collapsed menu strip is 80 px in the brief and 12 px in the accepted shell file, and was drawn as the shell file has it; and the green bar under the current navigation item is below 3 : 1 on white, so it is never the only cue. The design is a proposal: Dean has not accepted it and no named version is saved yet. The file's link is not recorded here (ADR-0050, ADR-0053).

Checked: every sheet was rendered and read during its batch and the defects found were corrected. On 12 November 2026 the whole file was scanned by routine: a structural pass and position audit on every page; an exposure scan over every text on all 143 customer sheets, which passed after five strings were reworded; an authority scan (no Accept, Approve, Sign, Pay, Book, Authorise, Confirm delivery, Grant or Edit control on a customer sheet except the one labelled D-024 proposal sheet); an evidence scan matching every reference and amount to the brief's dataset, with one assumed reference and two worked-out invoice lines recorded as assumptions; and a contrast table of 22 colour pairs, 21 of which pass. Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`. Not done: times, dates and names were checked by eye, not by routine; a person's keyboard, screen reader, zoom and device review; prototype links; dark mode. Not in scope: the design register and per-route page guidance (a separate reviewed change), and any application code, route, migration, permission, identity provider, email or MYOB integration. No customer has seen or used any of this, CPA-01 to CPA-16 remain Not run, and every record, person, value and date shown is synthetic.
