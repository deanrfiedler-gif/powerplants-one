End-to-end scenario TS-01 found that a project cannot hold its field work or reach Finance: a work order names its project only in free text, and Finance handoffs are per work order (finding F-02). Dean answered four questions on 30 October 2026, choosing each recommendation: a typed project link on work orders, milestone claims that Finance approves and MYOB processes, field hours and parts on fixed-price projects as cost evidence only, and no retentions for now.

The [decision record](../decisions/project-delivery-billing.md) sets rules R1–R4, a worked TS-01 example and build increments B1–B4 (typed work-order link, claim schedule on the commercial basis, a `ProjectClaim` Finance handoff kind, and the TS-01 update). The detailed rules are agent decisions under Dean's standing order. This contribution is documentation only: no application, migration, grant or Finance policy change. Real milestone schedules, tax invoice content and MYOB document types stay with Finance under D-017.
