---
title: Quotation detail design accepted
date: 2026-10-23
---

Dean accepted the Figma design for Quotation detail (`/estimating/quotes/[id]`), the hub of one quotation across its life, after an audit and polish pass: "I accept the Quotation detail design with your recommendations, including D1–D16 and Q1–Q11. Lock it in." The [design decision](../decisions/quotation-detail-figma-design.md) (PPO-ES05-QUOTATION-DETAIL-FIGMA-DEC) records departures D1–D16 and the answers to Q1–Q11: the shared record layout with an eight-step lifecycle bar of dated facts; a primary action that follows state while every decision stays on the release, response and conversion pages; the quote view tracking "Customer views" line and Details, shown only to the sender, quotation owner and deal owner and "Not tracked" until the privacy notice is confirmed; send outcomes from ADR-0059 kept apart from views; the A1 follow-up with the 5-working-day suggestion going to the deal owner; and, from the audit, a linked-records chain (UXB-06), a Customer page preview, a revision compare (UXB-18) and a History tab. The story uses the Deal record's SYN-PPO-QUO-000188 (Q1); the Deal record's 7 October history line and the register's SYN-QUO-0079 are not edited. Twenty-two icon-free captures with hashes are retained in [quotation-detail-design-r01](../reference/ui/estimating/quotation-detail-design-r01/README.md), and the `route:/estimating/quotes/[id]` register entry and design page cite them. In Figma the capture copies sit on the Captures page from y = 410,000, the File index has a row after Quotations, and the four new page parts moved to the Components page (Estimating parts).

Checked: every text is on PPO text styles with bound colours; no frame overlaps or sits outside its section; no clipped text; 777 icons in the capture copies were swapped to the placeholder, with none left. Not done: the prototype was not played in Present mode, and the named Figma version "Quotation detail — shared record layout, accepted" waits for Dean. The page is not built; customer views need QV-S1 and real send outcomes need ADR-0059 and QV-S2. No application code, route, migration, permission or parent ID changes.
