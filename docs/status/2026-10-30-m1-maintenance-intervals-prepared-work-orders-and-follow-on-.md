title: M1 maintenance intervals, prepared work orders and follow-on requests
date: 2026-10-30
---

Maintenance plans can now use SixMonthly and Yearly intervals as well as Monthly and Quarterly, still generated in runs of at most 12 months inside the agreement term, so a Yearly run yields one or two dates. Prepare owned Service request now also creates a Draft work order in the same operation, through the work order create and draft-scope rules: one task per plan task with its exact wording and kind, the occurrence's equipment on each task, and coverage from the Covered entitlement. A person still adds authority evidence, exclusions and the Finance route and authorises it. An occurrence received as Partial offers Prepare follow-on Service request, which raises only the outstanding tasks with a current Covered entitlement for the same original due date, one follow-on at a time; a completed follow-on completes the occurrence and the original request, report and result stay unchanged. Migration 0123 records the prepared work order and the Partial result a follow-on follows on the maintenance request, with database guards. Preparing maintenance work now also needs `service.work_order.edit`; no capability or grant was added. See the [maintenance contract](../contracts/maintenance-warranty-api.md#recurrence-and-service-receiving), the [Service API M1 amendment](../contracts/service-api.md#m1-maintenance-prepared-work-order-amendment--30-october-2026-implementation-in-review) and the [verification note](../testing/evidence/maintenance-m1/README.md).
