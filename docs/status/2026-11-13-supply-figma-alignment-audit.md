Dean asked for an audit of the Supply module's Figma page set, so that the file can be aligned with the accepted shell, the shared addenda, its own recorded rules and the other module files before it is accepted or built. The audit was read-only in Figma: no file was edited, moved, renamed, commented on or versioned. The [audit record](../decisions/supply-figma-alignment-audit.md) (PPO-SUPPLY-FIGMA-ALIGNMENT-AUDIT) holds the method, the alignment matrix, 31 findings (8 Major, 17 Minor, 6 Polish; none is a Blocker because the file is a proposal), the facts that differ across files, five questions for Dean and, as Appendix A, a paste-ready alignment batch for the next Supply build session. The Majors are a person who does not exist under Addendum A3 (Kim Patel), supplier claims in the progress-claim family (SYN-CLM- for SYN-SCL-), the Block C dispatch SYN-DSP-000061 differing from the Customer portal (dates, notice and zone), the SYN-ECR-000014 source line differing from the Engineering file, the word for Robin Hale's withheld Finance-trust view, phone record headers drawn instead of instanced, gendered pronouns, and 32 px phone targets. The structure is sound: every sheet sits on the template grid with a matching label, and the desktop shell is fully instanced and matches the accepted menus word for word.

Checked: all 15 scope pages, 233 sheets and 15 notes cards were extracted by script and every visible text node tested; all 233 node IDs and codes in the [Supply record](../decisions/supply-module-refined-ui-proposal.md) resolve to the same sheets; the Supply menus, top bar and rail were compared with the accepted Shell, and the top bar and rail pixel by pixel; one Engineering sheet and one Customer portal sheet were read for the handovers; the Library was read for component names and drawer widths. Repository checks: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`. Not checked: the addenda as text (A3 was read through the records that cite it), the Documents & messages, Estimating, My Work, Service, Products, Settings and Reporting files, contrast and one-line cells beyond the file's own audit, and any keyboard, screen-reader or device review. No other record, no design register entry, no code, route, migration, permission or integration was changed. Every record, person, value and date is synthetic.
