Dean accepted the Figma design for the Settings hub around AD-02, business configuration and policy publication (WS-D6), after an audit and polish: "I accept the Settings AD-02 design with your recommendations, including D1–D8 and Q1–Q6." The [design decision](../decisions/settings-hub-figma-design.md) (PPO-AD02-SETTINGS-HUB-FIGMA-DEC) records departures D1–D8 and answers Q1–Q6: a review by the proposer is refused as well as a publication by the reviewer, the hub is `/admin/settings` and Business policies `/admin/policies` with no capability of the hub's own, field readiness and working hours join the publication path one family at a time, Publish version N is primary only for a current publisher with a complete review, policies take no readable reference, and the History and Affected work tabs follow as amendments. Thirteen icon-free captures with hashes are retained in [settings-hub-design-r01](../reference/ui/settings/settings-hub-design-r01/README.md). The design register page for `scope:AD-02` cites the frames and captures. In Figma the page sits under "— Administration —" after Users and access, the File index has its row, and the capture copies sit on the Captures page from y = 360,000.

Checked: no Font Awesome instance or stray vector is left in the capture copies, no text colour differs from its bound variable, and no copy carries a prototype link. The named Figma version "Settings — accepted" was saved on 23 October 2026 at Dean's "Yes, save the named Figma version "Settings — accepted" and finish locking in the AD-02 design." Not done: the page is not built, and the app still lists Settings as unavailable. No application code, route, migration, permission, grant, policy value or parent ID changes.
