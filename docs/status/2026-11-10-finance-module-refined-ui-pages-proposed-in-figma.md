Dean asked for the complete, refined UI page set for the Finance workspace to be designed in a new, empty private Figma file, "PPO - Finance module (proposal)", composed inside the accepted shell and organised with the page template adopted for the Sales file, following his brief of 10 November 2026 and Addendum A3 (persona and story reconciliation). The [proposal record](../decisions/finance-module-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-FINANCE-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) holds the sources, the conformance declaration per scope (FN-01 to FN-06, Finance exceptions, the Finance extract of RP-05 and Finance settings), the sheet inventory with Figma node IDs, the rules applied, the assumptions and proposed departures, and what is not drawn. Finance is drawn as the review-and-control layer over MYOB, the book of record: no sheet posts, pays, resends or edits a MYOB document; every amount states its basis and its read; a figure that cannot be stated reads in words, never as zero. This fragment claims the work with a draft pull request while the pages are being drawn: the foundations (page template, colour variables, local copies of the shell chrome, kit and icons, the record header toolbar) and the Finance handoffs page are in the file; the remaining scope pages are in progress and the record's inventory and verification sections will be completed in the same pull request. One departure is recorded for Dean's attention: the accepted Shell draws the collapsed docked menu as a 12 px strip with 1300 px of content, so the pages follow that rather than the brief's "80 px" reading. The file's link is not recorded here (ADR-0050, ADR-0053). Design only: no application code, route, migration, permission or integration; design acceptance is Dean's and has not been given; every record, amount and MYOB figure is synthetic.

Checked: the Finance handoffs sheets were rendered and inspected, and a structural and position pass over that page found no overlap, a label on every sheet, no wrapped register cell and Roboto as the only font family. Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`.
