title: Finance module refined UI pages proposed in Figma
date: 2026-11-10
---

Dean asked for the complete, refined UI page set for the Finance workspace to be designed in a new, empty private Figma file, "PPO - Finance module (proposal)", composed inside the accepted shell and organised with the page template adopted for the Sales file, following his brief of 10 November 2026 and Addendum A3 (persona and story reconciliation). The file now holds 222 sheets: 221 on ten scope pages (Finance handoffs FN-01, Customer accounts FN-02, Finance exceptions, Claims and obligations FN-04, Project financial performance FN-03, Cash outlook FN-05, Measure definitions and reconciliation FN-06, the Finance view of Supply & Finance trust RP-05 with a proposed Finance Pulse, the interaction-states specification and the Finance settings pages), each with desktop, dialog and panel, state, tablet and phone sheets and a Flows and notes card, plus the accepted Finance flyout menu redrawn in this file's story. The [proposal record](../decisions/finance-module-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-FINANCE-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) holds the sources, the conformance declaration per scope, the sheet inventory with Figma node IDs, the rules applied, assumptions A1 to A18 and the audit. Finance is drawn as the review-and-control layer over MYOB, the book of record: no sheet posts, pays, resends or edits a MYOB document; recorded, approved, billable and posted stay four quantities and prepare, review, process and reconcile four duties; every amount states its basis and its read; a figure that cannot be stated reads in words, never as zero. The audit lists twelve corrections already made in the file, refinements for Dean (the main one: white on the accepted record header's green primary measures 3.57:1, below 4.5:1, a proposal for the Shell owner), gaps, all twelve innovation candidates drawn, eight questions and a recommended order. Two departures are recorded for Dean's attention: the collapsed docked menu follows the accepted Shell's 12 px strip rather than the brief's "80 px" reading, and two of the brief's figures are kept as their lines add up (Glasshouse 4 value to date A$191,340.00; scenario W11 cumulative +A$269,007.02). The file's link is not recorded here (ADR-0050, ADR-0053). Design only: no application code, route, migration, permission or integration; design acceptance is Dean's and has not been given; a named version is his to save; every record, amount and MYOB figure is synthetic.

Checked: every sheet was rendered and inspected; scans over every page found no overlap at any level, a label on every sheet, no wrapped register cell, no cut amount or notes text, aligned grid columns, Roboto as the only font family, no forbidden control and nothing loose, and every summed total recomputes to the cent; the contrast of 60 colour pairs was computed from the PPO colour variables (56 pass, 4 reported). Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check`, `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py` and `python3 scripts/check_naming.py`.
