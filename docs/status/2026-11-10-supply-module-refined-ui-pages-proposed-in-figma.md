title: Supply module refined UI pages proposed in Figma
date: 2026-11-10
---

Dean asked for the refined UI pages of the Supply module (the "Supply chain" workspace) to be built in a new private Figma file, "PPO - Supply module (proposal)", inside the accepted shell ("PPO - Shell", named version "Shell refined concept — accepted", 8 November 2026) and to at least the standard of the Sales module file, so that Supply has one consistent, reviewable design set before any page is built. The [proposal record](../decisions/supply-module-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-SUPPLY-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) lists the 233 sheets with their Figma node IDs on 15 scope pages: Material demand and the proposed Supply Pulse, Purchasing, Inbound shipments, Receiving, Inventory, Workshop builds, Kitting and controllers, Bench testing and returns, Dispatch and delivery, Returns and claims, Material change impact, Service stock custody, the Supply extract of Insights, Interaction states and Administration. Each page carries desktop sheets at 1440, dialogs and panels, states, tablet at 1024, phone at 390 and 320, and a notes card. The record holds the nine-field conformance declaration with the ownership of each scope, the rules applied, assumptions and proposed departures A1 to A21 (among them the proposed Workshop menu entry, the Supply Pulse, the phone bar, the two illustrative limits and the capability assumptions), the audit with its corrections, refinements, gaps, feature candidates and polish items, and the open questions. MYOB Acumatica stays the book of record on every sheet: nothing drawn posts, orders, reserves or moves stock, and every send shows one of five states. The eight questions in Dean's brief were answered by their stated defaults under his standing instruction and can be reversed. The design is a proposal: Dean has not accepted it and no named version is saved yet. The file's link is not recorded here (ADR-0050, ADR-0053).

Checked: representative sheets of every page type were rendered and read during each batch; a structural pass and a position audit over all 15 scope pages found every sheet inside its section with a matching label above it, no overlap at any level, no wrapped text in a register cell and Roboto as the only font family, and removed one loose leftover cell; the contrast of every text and control-edge pair in use was computed from the PPO colour variables (lowest text pair 5.14:1, lowest control edge 3.32:1; white on brand/green-deep is 3.57:1 and is not used on a Supply sheet). Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`. A second pass the same day, after the first record was merged, scanned every text on all 233 sheets: no weekday and date pair is wrong and no unknown person is named; it fixed 43 clipped texts on 31 sheets, corrected SYN-RCT-000091 to inspected 2 EA so the accepted Receiving rule D6 holds, removed a total of expected credits from RT12, applied refinements R1 to R3, and a re-scan found no clipped text left (section 11 of the record). Not done: a row-by-row proof of every quantity against the dataset, prototype links, a person's keyboard, screen reader, zoom and device review, the design register and per-route page guidance (a separate reviewed change), any application code, route, migration, permission or MYOB integration. Every record, person, value and date shown is synthetic.
