---
title: Phone identity changes on record pages wait for the record row
date: 2026-10-28
---

The P01–P11 browser-mobile proof (`npm run test:browser -- --config=playwright.compiled.config.ts --project=mobile-chromium`) timed out intermittently in `openPhoneMenu` ([`tests/helpers/phone-menu.ts`](../../tests/helpers/phone-menu.ts), line 20). It waited 5 s for the "More" dialog and found none. Three runs failed this way. `tests/browser/planner.spec.ts:504` (P05 project requests) failed on PR #719's head 3401bebf (26 October) and on PR #729's head 3765e195 (27 October). `tests/browser/packs.spec.ts:210` (P06 complete workbench) failed on PR #731's head e6b7f163 (28 October). Both failed in the spec's own `identity()` step, straight after `page.goto` of a record page: the appointment in P05 and the job pack in P06.

- **Cause.** A record page shows the phone list row, with Menu on the left, until the page's own read returns and publishes the record identity. The row then becomes the record row, with Back where Menu was ([application shell notes](../design/development/components/application-shell.md), "Records"). The page starts that read only once the session is ready. `identity()` waits only for the session read (`aria-busy="false"`), so it often pressed Menu while the record was still loading. When the record arrived between pointer down and pointer up, React replaced Menu with Back. The page-side log shows the sequence: `pointerdown` on Menu's icon; Menu removed and Back added (`data-phone-header` list → record); `mousedown` on `#shell-header`; `pointerup` and `mouseup` on Back's icon. The browser sent no `click`, Menu's handler never ran and the dialog stayed closed. Playwright checks its hit target only on the first pointer event, so it reported the click as done. The application's identity and menu rules were not involved.
- **Change.** After opening the record and before `identity()`, `planner.spec.ts` and `packs.spec.ts` now wait on phones for `#shell-header` to have `data-phone-header="record"`. `equipment.spec.ts` and `maintenance.spec.ts` already wait this way before they open the page guide (commit 1b3efa039). The identity is then changed from the tab bar's More, which the row change does not replace. The shared helper is unchanged. It cannot tell whether a page will gain a record row, and on a list page a wait for one would never end. No timeout, retry or assertion was removed or relaxed, and the application is unchanged.

Checked locally on 28 October 2026: Linux container, compiled application (`npm run build` of this branch), disposable PostgreSQL 16 `ppo_synthetic_test` restored from a fresh migrate and seed of main before each run. Chrome 156 is not installed here. The browser and the server's document renderer used the pre-installed Chromium 153.0.8010.12 through a local-only override, which is not committed.

- **Unchanged tests.** The two tests passed all 4 targeted mobile-chromium runs: twice alone, once under four busy loops, and once after `output-workspace.spec.ts` (whose tests precede P06 in CI) with all of `planner.spec.ts`. They also passed in a full mobile-chromium run of unmodified main. A failure needs the record to arrive within the few milliseconds of one press, so a handful of full runs does not reproduce it.
- **Harness.** A local-only harness repeated the failing step: open a record page, then run the specs' `identity()` steps through the unchanged shared helper, switching between the coordinator and the assigned technician. It logged pointer, mouse and click targets in the page, whether Menu's handler ran, the row changes and the dialog's `open` state. Without the wait, the "More" dialog stayed closed in 5 of 360 iterations: 4 of 140 on the P05 appointment with no load, 0 of 60 on it under four busy loops, 0 of 60 on it under 4× renderer CPU throttling, and 1 of 100 on a P06 job pack. Menu was pressed on a list row in 201 of the 360; under throttling the record always arrived first. The row changed mid-press in 5, and those are the 5 that failed, each with the sequence above. With the wait, the dialog opened in all 260 iterations (100 on the appointment, 60 on it under four busy loops and 100 on the job pack), and every press was on the tab bar's More.
- **With the change.** The two tests passed 4 of 4 targeted mobile-chromium runs (3 alone, 1 under four busy loops) and 1 desktop-chromium run. They also passed in the full mobile-chromium suite in CI's order (649 tests, 1.2 h), as tests 437 and 454, the positions at which they failed in CI. That run had 561 passed, 78 skipped and 10 failed. No failure passed through the phone menu helpers, and the full run of unmodified main failed the same 10 tests. Run alone, 8 of the 10 passed on both main and this branch. The other 2 failed on both in the server's document rendering, which here uses the local Chromium override: `estimate-reviews-register.spec.ts:235` and the "pack" case of `offline-acceptance.spec.ts:532`. The specs that use the phone menu helpers, directly or through `quality-browser.ts` and `service-journey.ts`, passed in the full run except four tests: `sh-platform.spec.ts:37` and `:256`, `quality.spec.ts:58` and `quality-journey.spec.ts:5`. Those four failed outside the menu, also failed in the main run and passed alone on both.
- `npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck`, the unit tests (1,167 passed), `npm run build`, `npm run studio:check`, `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py` and `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` passed.
- Not run: the Chrome 156 channel that CI uses, and the other browser lanes.

Open:
- Other specs also change the identity straight after opening a record page and are not changed here. They are `work-orders.spec.ts:92` (in the harness, 1 of 30 presses still landed on the list row), `reports.spec.ts:1001` and `email-calendar.spec.ts:173`. So is `openIdentityControls` in `tests/helpers/quality-browser.ts`, which presses Enter on the same opener for the P09–P11 journeys.
- A person who taps the top-left control just as a record loads can also lose the tap, or reach Back instead of Menu. The shell notes describe the list row until the record loads as intended, so any change there is a design decision.
