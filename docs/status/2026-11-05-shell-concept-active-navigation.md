---
title: Desktop concept active workspace follows the phone navigation style
date: 2026-11-05
---

Dean directed the private Figma desktop trial to adopt the phone's slate active background and green accent, with the 3 px stripe on the left. Both rail widths now use a square band and white active icon/Medium label. The expanded rail preserves a quieter current-page marker. A separate state reference distinguishes hover and keyboard focus, including focus on an already active workspace.

All 62 desktop states retained their selected workspace, x=36 icon alignment and prototype reactions. The 32 phone layouts were unchanged. Actual-size renders and sampled contrast checks are recorded in the [decision](../decisions/shell-concept-prototype.md#adopted-desktop-active-workspace): white on active slate 11.07:1; green on active slate 4.60:1. The [working shell master](../design/development/systems/shell.md) and [consumer binding](../design/development/component-bindings/2026-11-05-shell-concept-prototype.md) distinguish the adopted design from application implementation and accessibility proof. No runtime, deployment or accepted review fingerprint changed.
