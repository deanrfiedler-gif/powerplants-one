The shared-records pages Dean asked for on 10 November 2026 are now fully drawn in the private Figma file "PPO - Customers, Sites & Equipment module", and the [proposal record](../decisions/shared-records-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-SHARED-RECORDS-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) replaces its claim version with the completed record. The file holds 184 sheets on 15 pages inside the accepted shell and on the Sales file's page template: 76 desktop, 32 dialogs and panels, 31 states, 16 tablet and 29 phone, covering Organisations (CS-01), People (CS-02), Stakeholders (CS-03), Sites (CS-04), Facilities and growing areas (CS-05), Access & readiness (CS-06), Account development (CS-07), Site surveys (CS-08), the nine Equipment scopes (EQ-01 to EQ-09), Interaction states and Administration. The record lists every sheet with its node ID, the nine-field conformance declaration per scope, the rules applied, sixteen assumptions and proposed departures, the open questions, and an audit (corrections, refinements, gaps, automation candidates, polish) with a recommended order. The file's Read me page carries the same index and audit. It remains a **proposal**: Dean's acceptance, the named version of the file (his to save) and the design register and page guidance (their own reviewed change once the design is accepted) are not done, and no application code, route, migration, permission, integration or deployment is created or authorised.

Three conflicts inside the brief are drawn on their recommended reading and need Dean's decision: the survey state counts add to 13 of 14 (drawn as Reviewed 10); the stage dates of backup SYN-BKP-000019 precede the applied date of configuration r2 (drawn as given); and "Tue 30 Jun 2027" is a Wednesday (drawn as Wed 30 Jun 2027). One refinement touches the shared kit and is proposed rather than applied: `line/strong` measures 1.3:1 and fails 3:1 as a control border, where `line/control` (3.32:1) passes. Not drawn: the Filters, Columns, Export and Saved view panels (the Sales file's are the pattern), eight small forms that are shown only inside larger sheets or not at all, tablet sheets for Test instruments and Administration, dark mode, 200 % reflow sheets and prototype links.

Checked: a scripted structural pass over all 15 pages after the last change found nothing loose, no section, sheet, label or notes-card overlap, no child outside its sheet, no register cell taller than one line, Roboto as the only font family and one uniquely coded label per sheet; every `SYN-` reference on every sheet was extracted and checked against the brief's dataset, Addendum A3.9 and the fillers ledger; the forbidden words appear only inside the rules that forbid them; contrast was computed from the colour variables and every text pair passes 4.5:1. Rendered and inspected by eye: Sites, Access and readiness, Equipment, Movement, Organisations and the first Stakeholders sheets as they were built, then People, an Account development dialog, three of the four Interaction states parts and one sample sheet each of Facilities, Site surveys and Bulletins and lifecycle. Not rendered by eye: Configuration backups, Test instruments, Administration, the page states and most of the tablet and phone sheets built on 11 November, because the Figma tool-call allowance ran out; a structural pass cannot see clipped text, poor wrapping or a wrong component state, so a full visual pass is recommended before acceptance. Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check`, `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py` and `python3 scripts/check_naming.py`. Every record, person, value and date shown is synthetic.
