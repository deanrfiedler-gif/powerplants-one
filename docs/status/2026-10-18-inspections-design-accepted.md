Dean accepted the Figma design for Inspections: the technician's capture at `/my-jobs/inspections` (FI-03) and review, defects and retests at `/service/inspections` (FI-04). Asked "Do you accept Inspections with the recommendations?", he replied: "I happy to accept and proceed with your recommendations." The [decision](../decisions/inspections-figma-design.md) (PPO-FI03-FI04-INSPECTIONS-FIGMA-DEC) records departures D1–D7 and the recommended answers to Q1–Q4. The review register becomes a grid with views by review state; the attempt uses the SV-06 record layout with tabs that include Readings, Defects and retests, and Outcome; the phone capture is one scroll with a submit summary sheet and an online-only notice. Twelve icon-free captures with hashes are retained in [Inspections design r01](../reference/ui/quality-site-assurance/inspections-design-r01/README.md), and the FI-03 and FI-04 scope and route page notes point to them.

Not done: the named Figma version "Inspections — accepted" waits for Dean's explicit yes. The File index row and page header were updated after acceptance, and three slips in the captures (the technician's initials and two phone states) were corrected before commit. The Conversations action in the top bar is part of a separate shell amendment that is still open. Nothing is built in this form and the build order is not decided. The captures are design compositions with synthetic data, not application captures, and set no UI baseline. No code, route, migration, permission, capability or parent ID changes.
