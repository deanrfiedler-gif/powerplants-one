Dean accepted the Figma design for PD-04, compatibility, replacements and lifecycle: "Accept all". The [design decision](../decisions/product-compatibility-figma-design.md) (PPO-PD04-PRODUCT-COMPATIBILITY-FIGMA-DEC) records departures D1–D14 and answers Q1–Q7:
- a relationship register as a grid, grouped by review conclusion;
- compare for up to four exact revisions, with differences in words and units never converted;
- a relationship record with criteria, evidence, owners, review and uses limited to what the viewer may see;
- Conditional only when every criterion is Met, so DP-25 against MD-20 stays Unresolved with a drafted adaptor-kit condition;
- a replacement chain with lifecycle source and date, feeding EQ-07 as evidence;
- Needs reassessment when a revision changes on either side.
