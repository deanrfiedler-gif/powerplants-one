Claude drew stage 1a of the [calling design paper](../decisions/calling-and-caller-id-design.md) in Dean's Figma file as a proposal, on the page "Calling and call logging · CS-01 · CS-02 · CR-01". It covers:

- the Log call sheet, built on Record outcome, as a desktop dialog and a phone sheet: from click to call, from Calls to log with several matches, by hand, next activity failed after the call saved, and Wrong number offering Number needs checking;
- Calls to log in My Work, desktop and phone, with Log, Skip and its reasons, a fifth summary cell, the empty state and a source-outage notice (R9);
- the shared Call control with ten states, and where it replaces the six `tel:` links;
- the phone numbers editor for people and for organisations' main numbers.

The [proposal record](../decisions/calling-figma-design.md) lists the frames and node IDs, departures D1–D8 and open questions Q1–Q8 with recommended answers. The [retained captures](../reference/ui/crm/calling-design-r01/README.md) are 19 icon-free PNGs. Every frame was checked with screenshots inside Figma; the prototype was not wired or played.

Dean has not accepted the design and no named version is saved. Nothing is built: stage 1c waits for acceptance and for the stage 1b server core, which proceeds in its own pull request.
