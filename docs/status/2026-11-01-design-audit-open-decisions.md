The Figma design file audit of 1 November 2026 listed seven possible pages that needed Dean's decision. Claude recommended an answer for each, and Dean said "Adopt all seven". The [decision record](../decisions/design-audit-open-decisions.md) sets them out: the phone app spike only, a question to the IT contractor about Teams, MYOB keeping stock authority, a simple planner day map after an architecture decision, no calendar month view, one My Work home, and naming who to ask on the denied page.

This contribution is documentation only, plus a pointer in ADR-0063. It changes no application code, migration, permission or Figma page, and it contacts no one. The phone accounts, the IT contractor's answers and the MYOB partner's confirmation remain open. MYOB capability, map pricing and phone behaviour have not been tested.
