Dean accepted the Figma design for EC-01, the connected inbox and record linking (`/email` and `/email/[id]`), after four audit and polish passes: "I accept the Emails EC-01 design with your recommendations, including D1–D8 and Q1–Q5. Lock it in." The [design decision](../decisions/emails-figma-design.md) (PPO-EC01-EMAILS-FIGMA-DEC) records departures D1–D8 and the answers to Q1–Q5: the reply composer stays an EC-04 proposal until an ADR settles sending; the rail item reads "Email" in the Sales, Projects and Service rails; the pilot mailbox scope stays; a follow-up never copies the body; and on phone the global actions move into the menu. Twenty-two icon-free captures with hashes are retained in [emails-design-r01](../reference/ui/email-calendar/emails-design-r01/README.md). The design register pages for both routes and `scope:EC-01` cite the frames and captures. In Figma the File index has an Emails row after the Deal record page, and the capture copies sit on the Captures page from y = 270,000.

Checked: no Font Awesome instance or stray vector is left in the capture copies, no text colour differs from its bound variable, no copy carries a navigation link, contrast passes for every text and background pair, and both flows were played in Present mode. Not done: the named Figma version "Emails — accepted" waits for Dean's explicit yes, the page is not built, and the built synthetic email page is unchanged. Open items: Dean asked for sent email tracking (opened and viewed) during the lock-in; it is drawn as amendment A1 for separate acceptance and depends on Q1. The accepted Organisations and Service requests designs disagree on the customer of SYN-PPO-WO-000040. No application code, route, migration, permission or parent ID changes.
