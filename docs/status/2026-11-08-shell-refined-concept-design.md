title: Shell refined concept design accepted
date: 2026-11-08
---

Dean directed a refined concept of the desktop shell in a new private Figma file, "PPO - Shell refined concept", and on 8 November 2026 accepted the recommended answers to its twenty-two open questions by choosing "Accept as recommended". The [design decision](../decisions/shell-refined-concept-design.md) (PPO-SHELL-REFINED-FIGMA-DEC) records the two pages (40 shell sheets A–AN and 25 workspace-menu sheets), the 36 shell components and 66 colour variables, the headline rules out of R1–R115, the differences from the built shell and the earlier accepted top bar and rail decisions, and the decisions themselves: a 48 px bar with a centred 448 px search and New on the drawer's edge, an 80 px rail one step lighter than the bar, one docked menu per workspace with nested page views, a persistent right strip with an always-overlaying 480 px drawer, a tenant chip with an amber demo signal, person colours on avatars and workspace accent chips.

Checked: every sheet measured for children outside frames, overlaps and wrapped labels after each change, and viewed in Figma; contrast computed for every text and marker pairing; the workspace menus built from `src/shell/navigation.ts`, the supply and engineering navigation lists and `register.json`. Dean saved the named Figma version later the same day ("It has been saved."; recommended title "Shell refined concept — accepted"). Not done: no logo-free, icon-free captures are retained; Q9 asks Dean for a mark-only logo; the application build is not authorised and waits on the backlog rule and its own plan. No application code, route, capability, grant, migration or token in `src/` changes.
