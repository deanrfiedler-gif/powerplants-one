---
title: Desktop shell adopts the larger Powerplants brand and right divider
date: 2026-11-05
---

Dean approved the larger branding with a light divider on the right edge only. The shared Figma header now pairs a 36 px symbol with an 18 px Roboto Medium wordmark. The divider meets the 240 px expanded rail edge, and the symbol centres over the rail icons at x=36.

Verification covered all 62 desktop frames across both rail states at 1024, 1280 and 1440 px. The logo, wordmark, border, search clearance, utilities and existing prototype links passed. The [design decision](../decisions/shell-concept-prototype.md#desktop-search-alignment-and-branding-study) identifies the shared header and the comparison with its previous appearance.
