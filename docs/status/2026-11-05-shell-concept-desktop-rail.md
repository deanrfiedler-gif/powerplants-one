---
title: Desktop shell logo and rail-footer direction adopted
date: 2026-11-05
---

Dean adopted the proposal to align the Powerplants symbol with the desktop rail icons and move expand/collapse out of the header into the rail footer. The private Figma trial now uses a fixed Settings/footer area below independently scrolling workspaces, matching arrow alignment in both widths and 44 px controls. Its 31 compact/expanded pairs preserve the current prototype screen and fixed record state. The 32 phone layouts and Menu controls are unchanged.

The trial now contains 96 reachable screens and 759 destination actions, with no dangling target. Source checks passed logo/icon/arrow alignment, reciprocal toggles and bottom anchoring on all 62 desktop states. Screenshot review corrected expanded Service content widths and Sales toolbar clipping. Player checks preserved the Sales preview on collapse and Service authorisation through expansion, Schedule handover and collapse. Tooltip copy is specified; interactive hover, preference persistence, keyboard and device behaviour require application proof.

The [decision record](../decisions/shell-concept-prototype.md#adopted-desktop-logo-and-rail-footer-direction), [working shell master](../design/development/systems/shell.md) and [consumer binding](../design/development/component-bindings/2026-11-05-shell-concept-prototype.md) retain the adopted direction and its exact private node references. This is design-record work in the existing draft review, with no runtime, deployment, reference-image publication, baseline fingerprint or complete shared-shell acceptance change. Earlier state-continuity and phone-coverage gaps remain open.
