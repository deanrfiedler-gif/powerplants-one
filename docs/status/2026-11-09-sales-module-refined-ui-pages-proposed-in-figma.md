Dean asked for refined desktop UI pages for the Sales module in a brand-new private Figma file, "PPO - Sales module (proposal)", composed inside the accepted shell ("PPO - Shell", named version "Shell refined concept — accepted", 8 November 2026) and using the trial "PPO - Shell concept" Sales sheets as reference only. The [proposal record](../decisions/sales-module-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-SALES-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) lists the 36 sheets at 1440 × 900 with their Figma node IDs across seven sections (Deals, Deal record, Leads and Sales Pulse, Tasks, Activities and Email, Handovers and aftercare, Contacts, customers, products and insights, Dialogs and states), the rules applied from his brief (one line per register cell with sideways scrolling; no second search box in the content area), assumptions A1–A10 and what is not drawn. Later the same day Dean answered "Accept A1–A10 as recommended"; section 9 of the record holds the acceptance, and the Figma page and sections were renamed from "(proposal)" to "(accepted 9 November 2026)". Dean then asked for an extensive audit of the pages; section 10 of the record holds it: five corrections made in the file, a journey-by-journey coverage matrix, refinements R1–R14, gaps G1–G12, feature candidates N1–N12 and polish items P1–P10, with a recommended drawing order. Dean accepted that order the same day ("Accept the recommended order and draw them") and section 11 records what was drawn: the text trim on every sheet, attention markers, signals strips, drawer quick actions, the Scope & sites tab, eight more dialogs, six state sheets, nine tablet and phone sheets, and N1–N3 as proposals; Dean then accepted N1–N4 as well ("Draw N1–N4 as accepted") and saved the named version "PPO - Sales module (accepted)"; section 12 records how N1–N4 were integrated into the accepted sheets, the reconciliation of the deal-attention counts across Deals, Pulse, Pipeline insights and the Filters panel, and that a further named version is needed to pin the N1–N4 state. The accepted set is 62 sheets with no proposal sheets. Dean then asked for the next batch ("Draw the next batch.") and, while it was drawn, whether the file lets users customise Sales features such as the Deals stage pipeline; section 13 records the 52 sheets added: the remaining Deal and Lead record tabs, the Sales brief Questions tab and successor r2, the aftercare dialogs, Person, Stakeholders and Site records with the New organisation and New person forms, the Administration section (pipeline configuration, saved views, import wizard, notification preferences, columns, export) and six customisation pages (settings hub, stage editor over a draft version with its publish dialog, attention-rule thresholds, pick-lists, record and board-card layout), an interaction-states and accessibility sheet, fifteen more tablet and six more phone sheets, and the refinement and polish items R4 to R8, R10, R12, R13 and P10 applied to existing sheets. The editable stage set on the stage editor is drawn as a proposed departure (N13) for Dean's decision. The file now holds 114 sheets in ten sections. The file's link is not recorded here (ADR-0050, ADR-0053).

Checked: every new or changed sheet was rendered and inspected; a structural pass over all 114 sheets found one dialog 5 px past its frame (corrected), no wrapped text in a register cell, no duplicate sheet labels and Roboto as the only font family; the contrast of every text colour pair in the PPO colour variables was computed and exceeds 4.5:1. Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`. Dean then saved the named version "PPO - Sales module (accepted) · batch 2", which pins N1–N4 and this batch; a position audit after the save found every sheet inside its section with its label above it and no overlap at any level. Dean then adopted N13 ("Adopt N13 and remove the Proposed label from the Add stage row"): the stage set is configurable through the draft-and-publish flow, the five accepted stages stay the published version, and section 13.5 records the sheets changed, and Dean saved the named version "PPO - Sales module (accepted) · batch 2 · N13". Two changes followed without redrawing a sheet, recorded in section 13.6: the derived 1024 tablet top bar was corrected to the accepted shell's 332 / 360 / 332 px zones (19 instances follow the component), and the single page was restructured into the page template of the design-to-build standard (Read me, — Accepted —, eight scope pages in rail order, Shell and kit, — Proposals —, Scratch), moving every sheet with its node ID unchanged; a structural and position audit over every page found no overlap, and a second audit of the Handovers page after Dean reported overlaps found none, so that report stays open until he confirms after a reload. Dean then re-saved the file under the same name, "PPO - Sales module (accepted) · batch 2 · N13"; the later of the two versions of that name is the build basis. Not done: prototype links, tablet and phone variants of the dialogs, the per-route page-guidance updates in `docs/design/development/pages/` (a separate reviewed change now that the design is accepted), the Sales adoption of the record header toolbar proposed in the Shell file (not accepted), any application code. Every record, person, value and date shown is synthetic.
