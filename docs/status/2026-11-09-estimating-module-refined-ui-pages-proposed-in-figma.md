Dean asked for the complete refined UI page set of the Estimating module to be drawn in a brand-new private Figma file, "PPO - Estimating module (proposal)", inside the accepted shell ("PPO - Shell", named version "Shell refined concept — accepted", 8 November 2026) and to at least the standard of the Sales module file, with the Design file's Estimating pages as content reference. The [proposal record](../decisions/estimating-module-refined-ui-proposal.md) (PPO-ESTIMATING-REFINED-FIGMA-PROP) lists the 283 sheets with their Figma node IDs across nineteen sections (Estimating Pulse and workload, Sales briefs, Estimation wizard and discovery, Configure & price, the four specialised workbenches with their template and registry, Rules studio, Cost sources, Estimates with the Excel import, Reviews & approvals, Quotations with the four-step conversion, the customer-facing quotation page, Outcomes and reference cases, Insights, 43 dialogs and panels, ten page states, the interaction-states and accessibility sheet, sixteen tablet and twelve phone sheets, and eight administration and customisation pages), the rules applied from his brief (one line per register cell with sideways scrolling; no second search box; the shell as the source of truth for chrome; rule-based gates with explanations; draft-and-publish customisation), assumptions and proposed departures A1 to A18, the audit (corrections made, coverage, refinements, gaps, new features N-E1 to N-E10 and polish, with a recommended order) and what is not drawn. The module is drawn under its decided name "Estimating"; the code, BP-04 and the Shell file still say "Estimating & quotation", and that rename is recorded as departure A1. Later the same day Dean said to proceed on the recommended header elements (a record stepper, a labelled Actions menu and a Reports menu, with back, undo, add, duplicate and a combined Issue & send left out) and asked whether his quoting record screen could be used; section 10 of the record holds those directions and the two quotation-record sheets drawn from them. The file's link and key are not recorded here (ADR-0050, ADR-0053).

Checked: every section was rendered and read from sample sheets after each build and after every repair; a structural pass over all 283 sheets found no direct child outside its frame other than the two intentional first-screen clips of the phone customer page, no register cell taller than one line, Roboto as the only font family and a label on every sheet; a position audit found every sheet inside its section with no overlap at any level and nothing loose on the page; a story scan reconciled 112 synthetic references to one truth per record; a date scan over 1,037 dated texts corrected weekday labels against the real 2026 calendar and found ISO dates only inside three imported file names; a literal-fill scan found only the 62 dialog scrims; the contrast of every text colour pair in the PPO colour variables was computed and tabled on the states sheet, with two pairs below 4.5:1 recorded as refinements. Repository checks run for this change: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`. Not done: Dean's acceptance and the named version (his to save), prototype links, tablet and phone variants of the dialogs, the Sales-file follow-ups, the per-route page guidance in `docs/design/development/pages/` (its own reviewed change once the design is accepted), any application code. Every record, person, value and date shown is synthetic.
