Dean accepted the Figma design for Travel review (PL-05, `/schedule/travel`) on 18 October 2026: "I accept the Travel review PL-05 design with your recommendations, including D1–D5 and Q1–Q2." The [decision](../decisions/travel-review-figma-design.md) (PPO-PL05-TRAVEL-REVIEW-FIGMA-DEC) records it, and the [retained captures](../reference/ui/service/travel-review-design-r01/README.md) are six icon-free PNGs with hashes.
- **Design.** One person's day as a route of depot, travel legs and visits, a day summary, conflicts named on the leg they affect, and a comparison of a different order that changes no booking. "Review booking change" hands the exact visit to Changes and follow-up (PL-04).
- **Recommendations adopted.** The route estimate stays "Unknown" with no map until a provider is chosen through its own decision (Q1). The page starts on one person, not all permitted resources (Q2).
- **Shell.** The proposed Conversations action sits left of the information icon; the shell amendment is a separate, open record.

Not done: the named Figma version "Travel review — accepted" is not saved and waits for Dean's explicit yes. The page is not built in this form and building it is not decided; the app still shows the built card list. The images are design compositions with synthetic data, not application captures, and set no UI baseline. No code, route, migration, permission, capability or parent ID changes.
