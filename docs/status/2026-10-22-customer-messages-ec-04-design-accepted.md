title: Customer messages EC-04 design accepted
date: 2026-10-22
---

Dean accepted the Figma design for EC-04, the outbound queue for customer notices, after two audit and polish passes, three phone header fixes and a play-through of the prototype: "Yes, I accept EC-04, including D8, with the recommended answers." The [design decision](../decisions/customer-messages-figma-design.md) (PPO-EC04-CUSTOMER-MESSAGES-FIGMA-DEC) records departures D1–D8 and the answers to Q1–Q4: a Customer messages group in the Email workspace at `/email/customer-messages` (proposed); the exact message beside the facts it was built from, sent only through a Confirm and send dialog that names the sender; a second person to approve and send M4; held, outcome-unknown and failed follow-ups with no resend; class switches with a required reason; and the compact phone header later adopted for every phone page. The sending note is optional except for M4 approvals, the shared mailbox address stays a placeholder until S4, the supply coordinator may send a dispatch notice he or she recorded, and there is no Outlook fallback in the app. Twenty-eight icon-free captures with hashes are retained in [customer-messages-design-r01](../reference/ui/email-calendar/customer-messages-design-r01/README.md), and the `scope:EC-04` register entry and design page cite them. In Figma the capture copies sit on the Captures page from y = 330,000, the phone frames use Shell / Phone header · compact, and the new Icon/comments now shows in the shared Conversations action, with the capture copies pinned to the placeholder.

Checked: the desktop, phone and phone 320 flows were played in Present mode and the fixes then checked in the file (78 links, none broken); no Font Awesome instance or stray vector is left in the 902-icon capture copies; no text colour differs from its bound variable; contrast passes except the shell's decorative breadcrumb separator. The named Figma version "Customer messages — accepted" was saved on 22 October 2026 at Dean's request and confirmed in version history. Not done: the page is not built and D-025 stages S3–S6 are not authorised. No application code, route, migration, permission or parent ID changes.
