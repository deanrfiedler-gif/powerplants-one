Dean accepted the Figma design for Findings and follow-up (SV-07) at the proposed route `/service/findings`: "I accept the Findings and follow-up SV-07 design with your recommendations, including D1–D8 and Q1–Q4." The [decision](../decisions/findings-figma-design.md) (PPO-SV07-FINDINGS-FIGMA-DEC) records it, and the [retained captures](../reference/ui/service/findings-design-r01/README.md) are nine icon-free PNGs with hashes. Findings sits in the Service review workspace, with an Escalations group for SV-08 in the same side menu. The register lists unresolved findings from five sources by state, identified by their source record; each finding shows what closes it in its own record and never closes here. Three new features are adopted in the design: a next action saved as one owned My Work activity (D5), recurrence on the same equipment and check within 90 days (D6), and a remote diagnosis handover to SV-08 (D7).

Not done: the named Figma version "Findings and follow-up — accepted" is not saved and waits for Dean's explicit yes. No route, read model or service exists, and building the page is not decided. Aligning the accepted SV-06 side menu with the shared Service review menu is open. No code, migration, permission, capability or parent ID changes.
