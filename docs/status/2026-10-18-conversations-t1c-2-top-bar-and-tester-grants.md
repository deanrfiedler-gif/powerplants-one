title: Conversations top-bar action, badge and hosted tester grants (T1c-2)
date: 2026-10-18
---

T1c-2 of Conversations is built on branch `feat/conversations-t1c-2`, after T1c-1 merged ([PR #575](https://github.com/deanrfiedler-gif/powerplants-one/pull/575)). A Conversations action now sits in the shared top bar, left of the information icon, on every page and in the phone header, and is current on `/conversations`. Its badge counts the threads the viewer takes part in with an unread message ("9+" above nine, none at zero, not on the bell), from a new `GET /api/v1/conversations/badge`. The accepted 320 px and 1024 px header departures are applied: at 360 px and narrower the phone header keeps 4 px sides and an 18 px title and the page guide moves into the identity menu; below 1200 px the builder's design workspace moves into the identity menu. Dean decided the same day: "Yes: give the hosted demo testers conversation.read and conversation.post as part of T1c-2, so I can use Conversations in the hosted demo." Invited testers now get both duties in the scope and validity of their deal and project read grants (rule T1-1), for new invitations and through the existing-demo upgrade; pack reviewers get neither. Nothing is deployed to the hosted demo: that stays Dean's operation. Two points wait for Dean: showing the action only to viewers with `conversation.read`, and whether an author's own message should count on their badge. The shared Figma `Shell / Top bar` and `Shell / Phone header` components and the icon-free T1 captures (the lock-in) wait for his go-ahead. CI, owner review and production readiness are separate. No migration, seed, capability or parent ID changes.
