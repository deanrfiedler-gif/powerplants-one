Dean accepted the Figma design for AD-10, user onboarding, learning and support: "Accept as recommended". The [design decision](../decisions/onboarding-support-figma-design.md) (PPO-AD10-ONBOARDING-SUPPORT-FIGMA-DEC) records D1–D15, departures X1–X6 and answers Q1–Q10:
- a short task path by job function, counted as tasks done, with no score and no requirement before access;
- scripted practice on labelled synthetic records that never touch real work;
- Ask for help routes requests to the PPO support owner, possible incidents to AD-09 and guide gaps to DK-07;
- feedback, and What's new by release and job function, in-app only until email delivery exists;
- an Owner view of counts, with KPI-15 only as its defined measure and its definition status;
- Dean holds the support owner role during the pilot, with one named back-up.

Twenty-five icon-free captures with hashes are retained in [onboarding-support-design-r01](../reference/ui/administration/onboarding-support-design-r01/README.md), and the design register page for `scope:AD-10` cites them.

The named Figma version "Onboarding & support — accepted" is awaiting Dean's yes.

Checked:
- Overlap, section-bounds and spacing scripts, and in-Figma screenshots.
- No Font Awesome instance or prototype link in the capture copies.

Not done:
- Present mode was not run, so the prototype links are untested in play. Keyboard, screen reader and 200% zoom were not checked.
- The back-up support owner is not named, and the KPI-15 definition, baseline and target are not approved.
- The page is not built, and stage 5 of the user guide direction needs its own go-ahead.

No application code, route, migration, permission, grant or parent ID changes.
