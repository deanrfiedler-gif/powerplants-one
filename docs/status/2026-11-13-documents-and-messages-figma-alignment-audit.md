Dean asked for an audit of the Documents & messages module's Figma file, so that the file can be aligned with the accepted shell, the shared addenda, its own recorded rules and the other module files before it is accepted or built. The audit was read-only in Figma: no file was edited, moved, renamed, commented on or versioned. The [audit record](../decisions/documents-and-messages-figma-alignment-audit.md) (PPO-DOCS-MESSAGES-FIGMA-ALIGNMENT-AUDIT) holds the method, the alignment matrix, 28 findings (7 Major, 17 Minor, 4 Polish; none is a Blocker because the file is a proposal), the facts that differ across files, six questions for Dean and, as Appendix A, a paste-ready alignment batch for the next build session. The Majors are the document pages drawn in a proposed Documents workspace where the accepted shell puts them in the Knowledge workspace with More current; the register page called "Document library" where the shell says "Document register"; the copied record toolbar's green primary at 3.57:1 where Dean has returned primaries to navy; quotation SYN-QUO-000214 drawn in the catalogue form with a Saturday issue date where the accepted Estimating file has Friday 30 October; the SYN-DSP-000061 delay notice an hour apart from the portal's (AEDT against AEST); gendered pronouns in two notes cards and the file's record; and 32 px phone controls copied from the shell's phone component. The structure is sound: all 213 sheets are on the page template with correct labels and signed-in people, and every recorded node ID resolves.

Checked: all eight scope pages, 213 sheets and eight notes cards were extracted by script and every visible text node tested; all 213 node IDs and codes in the [Documents and messages record](../decisions/documents-and-messages-refined-ui-proposal.md) resolve to the same sheets and appear in the Read me index; the docked menus, top bars, rails, right strips and record toolbars were compared with the accepted Shell's Knowledge menus, More panel and components; one Customer portal sheet was read for a handover; the Library was read for its pages and, through its records, its components, drawer widths and button states; contrast was spot-checked from the variables. Repository checks: `python3 scripts/build_status.py --check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`. Not checked: the addenda as text, the other module files beyond their records, one-line cells and clipping (no screenshot was taken), and any keyboard, screen-reader or device review. No other record, no design register entry, no code, route, migration, permission or integration was changed. Every record, person, value and date is synthetic.
