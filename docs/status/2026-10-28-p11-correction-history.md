---
title: P11 journey opens technician History only after the field page settles
date: 2026-10-28
---

The P11 selected UI journey (`tests/browser/quality-journey.spec.ts:5`) timed out intermittently in the compiled-application browser lane (desktop-chromium, shard 2/2) on 27 October 2026: main 4d4e4d9c, PR #722 (ba58f51e, passed on re-run) and PR #729 (3765e195). Its "History" click completed, and it then waited 15 s at `service-journey.ts:424` for "Correct this observation entry".

- **Cause.** The assigned technician reaches the field page through the report's client-side link, and the journey clicked History as soon as the tab appeared. Scrolling the tab into view compacts the timer header (`#ppo-work-timer .head`, `data-compact`), which moves the tabs up a frame or two later. Playwright checks its hit target only on the first pointer event of a click. When the header compacted between pointer down and pointer up, pointer down reached History and pointer up reached the Capture panel beneath, so the browser sent `click` to their common ancestor `#field-execution`. History's handler never ran and its panel stayed hidden, so its correction buttons never entered the accessibility tree, while Playwright reported the click as done. The application's evidence, identity and correction rules were not involved: the entry was present, current and the technician's own.
- **Change.** A new helper, `technicianHistory` in [`quality-report.ts`](../../tests/helpers/quality-report.ts), follows the link while observing this actor's `GET /api/v1/my-jobs/<id>` and `/timer` reads. It waits for "Loading permitted records…" to clear, scrolls History into view and waits for the compacted header, as the journey already does before its pack acknowledgement. It then clicks once and asserts `aria-pressed="true"`. [`service-journey.ts`](../../tests/helpers/service-journey.ts) uses it in place of the two bare calls. No timeout, retry or assertion was removed or relaxed, and the application is unchanged.

Checked locally on 28 October 2026: Linux container, compiled application, disposable PostgreSQL 16 `ppo_synthetic_test` restored from a fresh migrate and seed before each run. Chrome 156 is not installed here, so the browser and the server's document renderer used the pre-installed Chromium 153.0.8010.12 through a local-only override, which is not committed.
- The unchanged journey passed all 6 full runs: alone, three times under four busy loops, once under 4× renderer CPU throttling, and once after 147 of the 167 desktop tests that precede it in CI's shard 2/2. Locally, Playwright ordered the 20 `my-work` tests after it.
- A local-only harness ran the journey, then repeated the failing step 80 times per run: open the returned report as the technician, follow the link and click History. It logged pointer events, `data-compact` and History's `aria-pressed` in the page. With the journey's original two calls, History stayed closed in 3 of 160 iterations (1 of 80 idle, 2 of 80 under four busy loops). Each of the 3 logged `pointerdown` on History, then `data-compact=1`, then `pointerup` on the Capture panel and `click` on `DIV#field-execution`. Capture was still pressed, the History panel was hidden and no correction button existed, which matches the CI symptom. In another 51 iterations Playwright's pointer-down check caught the shift and retried. With `technicianHistory`, History opened in all 160, and every iteration compacted before pointer down.
- Journey with the change: passed 7 of 7 full runs. That is 3 plain desktop-chromium runs after merging main 9a39e750, 3 desktop runs inside the harness, and 1 mobile-chromium run. `field.spec.ts` and `reports.spec.ts`, the other specs that click "Correct this … entry", passed on desktop-chromium (6 tests).
- `npm run lint`, `npm run typecheck`, the unit tests (1,165 passed), `npm run build`, `npm run studio:check` and `python3 scripts/check_foundation.py`: passed.
- Not run: the Chrome 156 channel CI uses, the full browser lanes, and the acceptance and Step 6 journeys, which reach this step through `serviceJourney` as well.

Open:
- `tests/browser/reports.spec.ts` (lines 459–462 and 678–681) follows the same link and clicks History in the same way through its own local helpers. It can lose the click the same way and is not changed here.
