Each invited tester receives the bounded Company A email.read/email.edit grants and a separate fictional mailbox containing the two existing sample enquiries. Personal meeting fixtures use the tester's initial setup day in Brisbane. Existing rows, dates, opportunity links and follow-ups survive repeat tester reconciliation. Adding a tester is an operator operation; no web startup or image update seeds mail or broadens grants. Retain the original Coordinator fixture for local demonstrations.

Messages remain accessible only to their owner under current permissions. Explicit linking exposes the selected follow-up Activity through normal shared CRM permissions without copying the email body into the Activity, audit or outbox. The calendar shows the signed-in actor's meetings and owned Activities. Creating an email follow-up preserves the opportunity's designated next Activity. This extends the existing synthetic adapter; Microsoft mail/calendar scopes, real synchronisation, sending, attachments and AI providers are separate work.
