## Adopted implementation boundary

Use the existing Next.js/React, PostgreSQL workspace transaction lock, expected versions, original-operation receipts, Activities primitives and exact-byte document store. No new dependency, workflow engine, notification service, external reporting or offline protocol. A standalone proposed HTML session cannot provide runtime authority. Keeping incidents inside inspection defects was rejected: a passing test and a completed task cannot decide an incident.

The authoritative incident has a UUID in `ppo.incidents`, immutable factual revisions and append-only events. It binds one real Service appointment, its work order/company/site, one exact scope item and equipment identity/configuration. Initial context is retained; current binding is separately assessed. Changed context requires explicit rebind and fresh review; previous holds remain effective against their original scope until explicitly reviewed closure. Matching titles never merge events. A repeated report retains its own identity and can explicitly link another permitted report; linking alone closes neither record.

Synthetic policy `incident-policy-1` defines Unknown, Observation, NearMiss and Incident classification; Unassessed or Assessed; Unknown, Routine or Urgent priority. These are coordination labels, never blame, competence, legal reportability or a risk assessment. All submitted unassessed reports conservatively hold their exact affected scope until a reviewer assesses applicability. Reporting and factual correction require no attendance, work-start or readiness success. Explicit reviewer hold/release decisions require reasons; only independently reviewed closure removes this incident's blocker. No automatic legal or operational decision follows a label.

Dedicated scoped capabilities are incident.read, incident.report, incident.review, incident.close and incident.sensitive. Reporting also requires a currently assigned field reader, or Service work-order edit authority. Read alone permits no mutation. Review/closure require their own current duty; no broad technician or administrator grant is added. Restricted factual details and evidence are readable only by a currently permitted sensitive reader or their original reporter; action owners receive operational summaries only unless independently permitted. Sensitive writers must retain their restricted-data authority. Scope and duty are rechecked on every command, file read and receipt recovery. No restricted facts are copied into global search, Activity summaries, counters, audit reasons, generic errors or output for ordinary readers.

Draft → Submitted → InReview / Returned / ClarificationRequired / OnHold → Accepted → Closed. Every edit advances an expected version and retains attributable history and a reason. Reopening retains all reviews/output and restores the hold. A correction invalidates acceptance; a source change makes acceptance stale. Triage assigns an accountable owner and due instant and explicit assessment/hold basis. Each corrective action has its own UUID, task instruction, owner and due instant. It creates a real RestrictedService Activity using the receiving domain's authority/insertion primitives. Reassignment must update the coordinated Activity through its supported command; divergence blocks acceptance. Activity Completed is necessary for its action review, never sufficient for evidence acceptance, incident closure or defect resolution.

Action owners append correction evidence; an independent reviewer accepts exact evidence and current source binding. Closure requires assessed classification/priority, accountable owner/due date, at least one accepted corrective action, all required action Activities completed, verified original evidence, current scope/configuration and exact latest independent acceptance. Overdue incomplete actions block closure; completed late work retains its dates. Reporter, action owner and evidence contributors cannot accept their own closure. A linked inspection defect must independently complete its required correction/fresh retest/acceptance before it can support incident closure; incident commands never edit the inspection engine.

Holds are applied to their exact work-order/scope-item/equipment combination, across visits of that work order. Appointment-wide readiness and Start refuse unresolved held work-order scope, including a removed required source whose applicability cannot be established; exact inspection release checks only its affected occurrence. Unknown or missing required sources fail closed. Recording a hold during attendance changes current authority and warns the user, but stops no equipment, cancels no attendance and edits no timer history. Factual P07/P08 evidence and recovery remain possible with ReviewRequired attribution. Existing delayed offline Start is revalidated on replay. Offline incident reporting, attachment, triage, action acceptance, closure and reopening are unsupported and never labelled saved. Clearing a hold removes only that blocker; scheduling, readiness, assignment and inspection restrictions remain independent.

Evidence uses bounded verified PNG/plain-text bytes and original hashes under existing storage, with explicit Operational or Restricted audience and immutable association/attribution/time. No general attachment repository or expanded media support is introduced. Missing/revoked evidence cannot support review. Corrected evidence is appended, never overwrites originals. Online server drafts survive reload/restart; unsaved input stays in page memory. The existing session command journal retains unchanged operation IDs/payloads for uncertain results, partitions identities and clears accepted payloads. No disconnected queue is introduced.

Closed outcomes bind exact incident revision, review, policy, affected scope, evidence hashes, action and linked-inspection history, exclusions and remaining work. The controlled bundle mechanism retains Internal HTML/PDF; output contains only operational information and explicitly excludes restricted facts. Restricted source bindings remain in the protected incident record. Reopening changes current applicability without rewriting issued bytes. Closure completes no appointment, work order, project, customer acceptance or Finance process. External notification, emergency response, equipment control and regulatory decisions remain unavailable.
