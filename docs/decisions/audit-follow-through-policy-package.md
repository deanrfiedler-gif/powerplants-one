## First implementation: CRM outcomes

Record the outcome through the current owner's scoped CRM edit authority and a strict versioned command. Retain the stage where it closed. Won requires a recorded acceptance/order-evidence description. Lost requires Price, Competitor, Timing or No decision; optional notes accompany the selected reason. The required operation reason can be the selected action description when no extra note is entered.

Commit the opportunity version, immutable outcome event, audit, original receipt and minimal internal outbox together. Won adds exactly one handover-due companion keyed by the opportunity; it does not replace the existing next Activity. Keep the closing owner, exact outcome event and acceptance evidence as the handover basis. The due record cannot claim a receiving owner, route confirmation or completed delivery.

Reject a fresh second outcome, reopening and stage movement after closing. Existing independently authorised Activity work and the recorded history remain accessible. Existing information/scope/next-action permissions are not enlarged by closing. The worklist defaults to Open, with explicit outcome filtering for historical Won/Lost records and the same server projection for Board/List.

Original accepted operations remain recoverable under their existing authority; no extra retries or new offline queue are introduced. H-03's later transfer change must prove the original CRM receipt before its narrow current-owner exception can apply.
