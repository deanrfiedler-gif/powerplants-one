## Architecture and alternatives

Reuse TypeScript/Next/PostgreSQL, native validation/history/impact command, shared permissions, workspace serialization, actor-bound journal and atomic receipts/audit/outbox. Add companion immutable events and scoped review links in migration 0065; no seed, capability, grant, identity, framework, dependency, service or deployment infrastructure. Preserve hashes of earlier command families. Reserve the proposal's native operation across families and recover under current authority before disclosure.

Reject a note-only workaround, mutable consent flags, silently reduced allocations, a duplicate inventory ledger and external receipt reversal. Blocking all reduced evidence would misrepresent the supported native transition. Broad fulfilment/custody/return corrections require their own contracts and are deferred. This one existing-fact correction with all native affected-demand decisions is the smallest useful executable slice.

Operational approval/signing, observation-age thresholds, unit conversions, item governance, commercial terms, pricing, tax/FX and ERP mappings remain Not configured. MYOB remains intended ERP authority, SharePoint owns business documents, native CAD retains authoring. Source presence, automated proof, visual review, owner acceptance and deployment remain separate.

Affected-demand receiving requires current source/linked read access and coordination authority on that Demand only. It does not require or grant coordination authority on the converted Demand or other affected Demands. The final native actor still needs every permission required by the actual native command. The receiving queue and original receipt lookup preserve this distinction.

Native Supply scope identity (including item and unit) remains immutable. Current independent OneOff conversions generate distinct item identities; they cannot be relabelled to create shared eligibility. Shared-supply proof uses matching native Demands and proves competing/referral replacement for the original converted target. Defensive cross-target reservation checks do not adopt catalogue mapping or demonstrate a multi-quotation common-item journey.
