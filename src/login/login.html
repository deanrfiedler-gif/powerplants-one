Help signing in

Use the work account that has been given access to Powerplants One.

I need access

Ask your Powerplants One administrator to arrange access for your work account. If you do not know who to contact, ask your manager.

Microsoft shows the wrong account

Choose another account on the Microsoft sign-in screen, then select the work account that has access to Powerplants One.

I forgot my password

Use the recovery options on the Microsoft sign-in screen, or contact your organisation’s IT support.

My access has been denied

Check that you selected the correct work account. If access is still denied, contact your Powerplants One administrator. Signing in to Microsoft does not automatically grant access to this app.

Access is managed by the Powerplants One administrator. Microsoft handles your account password and verification.
